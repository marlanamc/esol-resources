Station 3: Health Literacy

1. The Medicine Label

Work with your group. Look at the label. Use simple English. Ask your teacher if a word is new.

Example: A typical label from a Boston pharmacy.

Read and Talk

  • 1. Look at the label. What is the medicine name? What is one question Maria could ask the pharmacist?
  • 2. The label says "Take 1 capsule 3 times daily." Maria takes the first dose at 8:00 AM. What are two more good times to take the medicine? Explain your answer.
  • 3. Maria feels better after 4 days. The bottle still has pills left. What should she do, and why?
  • 4. Maria finds this bottle next year. What date should she check before taking the medicine? What should she do if the date has passed?
  • 5. The label says "May cause drowsiness" (sleepy). Maria drives a school bus. What is the safest plan before she drives?
  • 6. Real Life: Maria is confused about the directions. Write one sentence she can say at the pharmacy.

2 — The Insurance Card (Simple)

Look at the card. You do NOT need to know all words. Focus on: member ID, phone number, co-pay, ER, urgent care.

Example insurance card.

Word on CardSimple Meaning
HMOA health plan type. Usually you choose one main doctor first.
PPOA health plan type. Usually you have more doctor choices, but it can cost more.
Co-payThe amount you pay at the visit.
Member IDYour insurance number.
Urgent CareFor problems that are important, but not life-threatening.
EREmergency room. For chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, and other emergencies.

Read and Talk

  • 1. Maria is at a doctor's office. What information on the card will the office probably ask for first?
  • 2. Maria is sick on Saturday, but it is not an emergency. Based on the card, which place may cost less: urgent care or the ER? How do you know?
  • 3. Maria paid a co-pay at the office, but later she got another bill in the mail. What is one possible reason?
  • 4. Maria does not understand HMO/PPO. What is the best first step: guess, ask a friend, or call the number on the card? Explain.
