Phone Call Boot Camp

You will make real phone calls in English today. Use this sheet the whole time.

The Phone Call Skeleton — Use Every Time

1
Say hello and your name "Hello, my name is ___."
2
Say why you are calling "I'm calling because ___."  /  "I need help with ___."
3
Give details / ask your questions Use the information on your scenario card.
4
Confirm and repeat back "So just to confirm, ___. Is that correct?"
5
Thank them and say goodbye "Thank you for your help. Goodbye!"

Before You Call — Get Ready

  • Read your scenario card all the way through
  • Know the name of the place you are calling (doctor's office, bank, hotel…)
  • Have your key details ready: dates, times, names, amounts
  • Have something to write with — you may need to take notes
  • Sit back-to-back with your partner. No looking at faces!

Phrase Banks

When you don't understand

  • "Could you repeat that, please?"
  • "Could you speak more slowly?"
  • "I'm sorry, I didn't understand."
  • "Could you spell that, please?"
  • "One moment — let me write that down."

For problems & complaints

  • "I think there may be an error."
  • "This is not what I expected."
  • "I would like to report a problem."
  • "I was told that ___."
  • "What can we do to fix this?"

For appointments & reservations

  • "I'd like to make an appointment."
  • "Do you have anything available on ___?"
  • "I need to reschedule."
  • "What time works best?"
  • "Can I get a confirmation number?"

For asking questions

  • "Could you tell me ___?"
  • "I'd like to know ___."
  • "How much does that cost?"
  • "How long will that take?"
  • "Is there anything else I need to bring?"

Your Two Roles

CALLER — You have the scenario card

  • Start the call: "Hello, my name is ___."
  • Say why you are calling right away
  • Cover all the bullet points on your card
  • Confirm important details before you hang up
  • Thank them and say goodbye

RECEPTIONIST — You know your workplace

  • Answer: "Hello, [place name]. How can I help you?"
  • Listen carefully — don't interrupt
  • Ask for details if you need them
  • Make up reasonable answers — be helpful!
  • Close the call politely

Example Call — Disputing a Bill

RECEPTIONIST: Hello, City Electric. How can I help you?
YOU: Hello, my name is Rosa. I'm calling because I have a problem with my bill.
RECEPTIONIST: I'm sorry to hear that. Can I get your account number?
YOU: One moment — let me write that down. Could you repeat that?
RECEPTIONIST: Sure. I need your account number, or the address on the account.
YOU: It's 42 Oak Street. My bill is usually $85, but this month it says $250. I think there is an error.
RECEPTIONIST: I understand. Let me look into that for you.
YOU: Thank you. So just to confirm — you will review my bill and call me back. Is that correct?
RECEPTIONIST: That's correct. We'll call within 2 business days.
YOU: Perfect. Thank you for your help. Goodbye!

Speed Round — How It Works

  1. You will get a scenario card. Read it before the round starts.
  2. Sit back-to-back. The CALLER starts when the teacher says go.
  3. Finish the call: hello → reason → details → confirm → goodbye.
  4. When the round ends, switch roles or rotate to a new partner.
