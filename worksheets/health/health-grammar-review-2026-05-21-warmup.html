1 — Parts of Speech

~5 min
Part of speechWhat it doesHealth example
NounPerson, place, thing, ideafluids, remedy, ailment
VerbAction or staterelieve, soothe, helps
AdjectiveDescribes a nouna minor ailment  ·  warm tea
AdverbDescribes a verb, adjective, or adverbquickly relieves pain  ·  works well
Focus — adjective or adverb? Adjectives describe nouns (a sore throat). Adverbs often end in -ly and describe verbs (works well, quickly helps). After linking verbs like feel / smell / taste, use an adjective: The tea smells good.

2 — Connectors

~5 min
WordWhen to use itExample
socause → resultI was thirsty, so I drank water.
becausegives the reasonI drank water because I was thirsty.
beforeearlier time / firstTake medicine before you eat.
althoughcontrast (but)Although the tea is bitter, it helps.

Tip: so = what happened next. because = why. before = earlier. although = surprising contrast.
