Part B · At the Doctor's Office

5 min · read with a partner

Read each dialogue out loud. Notice the bold words — should = advice, must / have to = required, need to = necessary.

Dialogue 1 — A sick child
Parent: My daughter has had a fever since yesterday. What should I do? advice
Doctor: She needs to rest and drink a lot of fluids. necessary
Parent: Can she go to school tomorrow?
Doctor: No. She must stay home until the fever is gone for 24 hours. It could be contagious. required
Parent: OK. Should I give her medicine for the fever?
Doctor: Yes, you should give children's Tylenol. Here's a prescription.
▶ Which rule is stronger — "She should stay home" or "She must stay home"?
Dialogue 2 — Annual checkup
Doctor: Everything looks good today. Your checkup is normal.
Patient: That's great. Do I have to come back soon? required?
Doctor: Not right away, but you should schedule your next appointment for next year. advice
Patient: What about the blood test? Do I need to fast before it?
Doctor: Yes, you must not eat anything for 12 hours before the test. required
▶ The patient asks "Do I have to come back?" — is the doctor's answer "yes, you must" or "it's a good idea"?
