Part B · Two Friends Catch Up

5 min · read with a partner

Read the dialogue out loud — one person is Lucia, the other is Ana. Notice the three grammar patterns in bold.

At a coffee shop after class
Lucia: Ana! I haven't seen you in a while. How are you?
Ana: Good! I'm trying to be healthier. I used to drink soda every day, but I stopped. past habit
Lucia: Wow, that's hard. Do you miss it?
Ana: At first, yes. But now I 'm used to drinking water instead. It took a few weeks to adjust. accustomed
Lucia: I should try that. I used to walk to work, but now I drive everywhere. past habit
Ana: Why don't you walk again?
Lucia: It's too far now. But I 'd rather walk than sit in traffic! preference
Ana: Me too. I 'd rather cook at home than eat fast food, but I'm still getting used to cooking for myself. preference + adjusting
Lucia: The transition is hard, but it gets easier. You're doing great.
▶ Find one sentence about a past habit, one about being accustomed, and one about a preference. Underline each.
