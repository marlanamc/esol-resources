Part C — Specialists & Referrals

10 min

A specialist is a doctor who is an expert in one part of the body. Most of the time you need a referral — a note from your PCP — before you can see one. The rules depend on your insurance.

1
You have a problem
that does not go away or that your PCP cannot fix
→
2

explain the problem; ask, “Do I need a specialist?”
→
3
PCP sends a referral
to the specialist’s office and to your insurance
→
4
Call the specialist
to make your appointment — bring your insurance card
