✦ ✦ Gerunds & Infinitives in Real Life ✦ ✦

May 14, 2026 · connects to the East Boston flyer on the front page

Gerund — verb + ing

  • After enjoy, keep, practice, avoid, finish:
  • I enjoy walking by the water.
  • As a subject:
  • Sleeping well helps me focus.

Infinitive — to + verb

  • After want, need, decide, plan, hope, try:
  • She plans to join the walking group.
  • To show purpose (why?):
  • I walk to reduce stress.

Activity 2 — Build a Healthy Sentence

4 min

Finish each line so it’s true for you (or for a fictional person). Try not to copy the example.

A. plan to + verb (purpose)

On Sunday, I plan to ____________________________

e.g. plan to walk · plan to call · plan to cook

B. enjoy + verb-ing

After a busy week, I enjoy ________________________

e.g. enjoy reading · enjoy walking · enjoy cooking

C. need to + verb

When I feel stressed, I need to __________________

e.g. need to sleep · need to call · need to breathe

D. verb-ing as subject

____________________ helps me feel calm and strong.

e.g. Walking · Cooking · Praying · Calling Mom
