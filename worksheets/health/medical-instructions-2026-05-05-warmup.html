Part B — Read the Prescription Label

8 min

Read the label carefully. Answer the questions — use complete sentences when you can.

CITY PHARMACY — (555) 018-2200
Patient:Maria Gonzalez
Drug:Amoxicillin 500 mg capsules
Take:1 capsule 3 times a day (every 8 hours) for 10 days
Prescriber:Dr. Chen
Date filled:May 1, 2026  •  Qty: 30
Refills:0 — No refills remaining
! Take with food • Finish ALL capsules even if you feel better • No alcohol
1. How many capsules does Maria take each day?
2. Maria feels better after 5 days and wants to stop. Is this OK? Why?
3. Maria needs more medicine next week. Can she get a refill? What should she do?
