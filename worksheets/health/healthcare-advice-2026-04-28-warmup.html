Part B · Fix the Bad Advice

6 min

Each sentence uses the wrong modal. Cross it out and rewrite the sentence with a better one.

should shouldn't must must not need to have to don't have to
1. You must drink water when you're thirsty. (Is this really required, or just good advice?)
2. You should tell the doctor if you're allergic to a medicine. (Is this just advice, or is it required?)
3. You don't have to go to the hospital if you have chest pain. (Really? Not necessary?)
4. You must eat an apple every day. (Is this a law, or a suggestion?)
5. You should bring your ID to the pharmacy to pick up a prescription. (Can you get it without ID?)
6. You must get a flu shot every year. (Is this a requirement for everyone, or just a recommendation?)
7. You don't have to finish all of your antibiotics. (Is skipping doses OK, or is it important?)
8. You should share your prescription medicine with a family member who has the same symptoms. (Is this just a bad idea, or is it actually dangerous/forbidden?)
