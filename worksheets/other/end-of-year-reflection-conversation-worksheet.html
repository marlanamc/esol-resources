End-of-Year Reflection Conversation

Focus: Present Perfect and Present Perfect Continuous

Grammar Reminder

Read first

Present Perfect: I have + past participle

Use for accomplishments, changes, and experiences.

I have learned new vocabulary. I have improved my pronunciation. I have become more confident.

Present Perfect Continuous: I have been + -ing

Use for repeated practice or something still continuing.

I have been practicing speaking. I have been trying to use English outside class. I have been working on pronunciation.

2. Group Conversation

15 min

Talk in a small group. Take turns asking questions. Write short notes from your group.

1.What have you learned this year?
2.How have you improved since September?
3.What have you been practicing the most?
4.What have you been trying to do better?
5.What has been difficult for you?
6.What have you enjoyed in class?
7.How have your classmates helped you?
8.What are you still working on?

3. Helpful Sentence Starters

Use while speaking

Use: I have...

  • I have learned...
  • I have improved...
  • I have become more confident with...
  • I have tried...
  • I have enjoyed...
  • I have helped...
  • I have made progress with...

Use: I have been...

  • I have been practicing...
  • I have been studying...
  • I have been working on...
  • I have been trying to...
  • I have been speaking...
  • I have been listening to...
  • I have been using English to...
  • I have been asking more questions.

4. Group Conversation: Progress

Round 2

Choose questions as a group. Everyone should answer at least two questions.

1.What English skill have you improved the most this year?
2.What is one thing you have learned from a classmate?
3.What have you been doing differently in English class?
4.What have you been practicing outside of class?
5.What have you become more comfortable doing?
6.What has helped your group learn together?

5. Group Conversation: Looking Back and Forward

Round 3

Use these questions for a final group discussion. Try to include one "I have..." answer and one "I have been..." answer.

1.What is one class memory you have enjoyed?
2.What is one challenge you have worked through?
3.What have you been trying to improve for next year?
4.How have you been using English in your life?
5.What advice would you give to a new student?
6.What do you want your group to celebrate today?

Board Cheat Sheet

This year, I have...

Something I completed, accomplished, changed, or experienced.

I have improved my speaking.

I have learned new grammar.

I have become more confident.

This year, I have been...

Something I practiced again and again, or something I am still working on.

I have been practicing speaking every week.

I have been trying to use grammar when I talk.

I have been speaking more in class.
