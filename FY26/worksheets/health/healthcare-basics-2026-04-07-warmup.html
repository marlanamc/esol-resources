Part B: Gerunds and Infinitives

8 min

Focus: Use the healthcare vocabulary from Part A to practice which verbs are followed by a gerund and which are followed by an infinitive. Read the examples, notice the pattern, and write your own sentence each time.

1) I enjoy / avoid / am considering ___ (verb + gerund)
  • I avoid coughing without covering my mouth.
  • I am considering seeing a cardiologist.
Your turn:
2) I want / need / plan ___ (verb + infinitive)
  • I need to see a pediatrician for my child.
  • I plan to call the doctor if my cough gets worse.
Your turn:
3) It is important / helpful / hard ___ (infinitive)
  • It is important to treat infected skin quickly.
  • It is hard to feel calm when you are depressed.
Your turn:
4) Stop / keep / finish ___ (verb + gerund)
  • Please stop coughing into the air.
  • She kept feeling depressed after the illness.
Your turn:
5) Learn / decide / remember ___ (verb + infinitive)
  • I decided to talk to a doctor about my inflamed knee.
  • Remember to tell the doctor if the area looks infected.

Dialogue 1

4 min
Scene: You are calling a clinic because you have had a bad cough for several days. You want to speak to a nurse and decide whether you should come in today.

Patient: Hi. I need to talk to someone about this cough. I have been coughing for five days, and now my chest feels sore.

Nurse: Do you have a fever?

Patient: No, but I want to stop coughing at night because I can't sleep.

Nurse: It is important to drink a lot of water and avoid going to work if you feel worse. The doctor may want to check if your throat is inflamed or infected.

Patient: Okay. I am considering seeing the doctor today because I need to feel better before tomorrow.

Nurse: Try to rest this afternoon, and keep drinking warm tea if it helps.

Patient: I will try to stay home and avoid talking too much at work tonight.

1) Find all the gerunds and infinitives in the dialogue.
2) Why does the patient want to see the doctor today?

Dialogue 2

4 min
Scene: You are calling a doctor's office for your child. He started feeling sick last night, and you want to make an appointment as soon as possible.

Parent: Hello. I need to make an appointment for my son. He has been feeling sick since last night.

Receptionist: What symptoms does he have?

Parent: He started coughing this morning, and the skin around his eye looks inflamed. I want to take him to a pediatrician as soon as possible.

Receptionist: We can see him at 2:30. If the area looks infected, the doctor may decide to give him medicine.

Parent: Thank you. I will keep watching him and try to keep him from touching his eye.

Receptionist: Please remember to bring his insurance card and avoid putting any cream on the eye before the visit.

Parent: Okay. He also needs to stop rubbing it, but that is hard for him.

3) Find all the gerunds and infinitives in the dialogue.
4) What two health problems does the parent describe?
