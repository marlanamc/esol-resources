Part B — Wellness Reflection Reading

8 min

Read the passage. Answer the questions — use complete sentences when you can. Notice the different verb tenses.

MY WELLNESS REFLECTION

I used to skip breakfast, but now I eat it daily. Last year, I started walking three times a week. While I was building this habit, I also learned to cook healthier meals.

Today I exercise regularly and I am working on better sleep. I have improved my health this semester, and I have been sustaining my routine for three months.

This summer, I will keep walking and I am going to grow my cooking skills. I hope the outcome is that I will sustain these healthy habits even when school is out.

1. What did the writer do in the past? Name two actions.
2. What is the writer doing now? What have they accomplished?
3. What will the writer do this summer? What outcome do they hope for?
