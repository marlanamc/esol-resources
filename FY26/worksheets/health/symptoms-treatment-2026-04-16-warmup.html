Part B · At the Doctor's Office

5 min · read with a partner

Read each dialogue with a partner. Notice: which sentences show a result (finished)? Which show duration (still happening)?

Dialogue 1 — Follow-up visit
Doctor: How are you feeling since the surgery?
Patient: Much better. I have recovered a lot. result
Doctor: Good. And the medicine?
Patient: I have been taking it every morning for three weeks. duration
Doctor: Great. Your liver looks much better on the new test.
▶ Which sentence means "I am still taking it"?
Dialogue 2 — Calling for results
Patient: Hi, I'm calling about my blood test. I have been waiting for the result since Monday. duration
Nurse: Let me check… Yes, your result has come in. result
Patient: Oh, thank you! Has my bladder infection improved?
Nurse: Yes, it has improved. But the doctor says you need to keep taking the medicine for the full duration — two more weeks.
▶ Which sentence means "I started waiting and I'm still waiting"?

Part D · Result or Duration?

3 min

Circle the correct sentence. Ask yourself: is it finished (result) or still happening (duration)?

1. The patient feels much better now.
a) She has recovered well. b) She has been recovering well.
2. He started the medicine on Monday. Today is Thursday. He still takes it every day.
a) He has taken the medicine. b) He has been taking the medicine since Monday.
3. Maria sent her blood to the lab a week ago. She doesn't have the answer yet.
a) She has waited for the result. b) She has been waiting for the result for a week.
4. The doctor checked his liver. Everything is normal.
a) His liver has improved. b) His liver has been improving.
5. Ana has a bladder infection. She started antibiotics 5 days ago. She still takes them.
a) She has taken antibiotics for 5 days. b) She has been taking antibiotics for 5 days.
