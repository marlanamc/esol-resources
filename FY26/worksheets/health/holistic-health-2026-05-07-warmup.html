Part C: Gerund or Infinitive?

7 min
Gerund  (verb + -ing)
Use for activities and experiences.
Use after: recommend, avoid, finish, keep, practice
The doctor recommended resting.
He avoids driving after medicine.
Infinitive  (to + verb)
Use for goals, plans, and decisions.
Use after: decide, want, plan, need, remind
Maria decided to call the nurse.
My goal is to exercise weekly.

Complete each sentence with the correct form of the verb in parentheses.

4. The pharmacist reminded David to avoid   (drive) after taking the medicine.
