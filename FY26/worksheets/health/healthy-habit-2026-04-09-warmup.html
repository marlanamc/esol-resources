Part B: Gerund or Infinitive?

7 min
🏃 Gerund  (verb + -ing)
Use for activities and experiences — things you do or did.
Use after: enjoy, avoid, quit, keep, finish, practice
I enjoy walking after dinner.
She quit smoking last year.
I avoid eating fast food.
🎯 Infinitive  (to + verb)
Use for goals and plans — things you want or decided to do.
Use after: want, plan, decide, need, hope, try
I want to drink more water.
We plan to join a gym.
He decided to sleep earlier.
