Gerund or Infinitive?

Independent Work • ~15 min • Use the reference box if you need help
Station 1
Gerund — verb + ing
Use after verbs about activities & experiences.
enjoy, avoid, quit, keep, finish, practice, consider, suggest, miss, mind, recommend, imagine, risk
I enjoy walking after dinner.
She quit smoking last year.
I avoid eating fast food.
Infinitive — to + verb
Use after verbs about goals, plans & decisions.
want, plan, decide, need, hope, try, agree, afford, expect, choose, refuse, offer, promise, learn, manage
I want to drink more water.
We plan to join a gym.
He decided to sleep earlier.
Activity 1 · Circle the Correct Form
~3 min
Circle the correct word to complete each sentence.
1. She wants ( eating / to eat ) more vegetables this year.
2. He decided ( moving / to move ) to a new city for work.
3. I enjoy ( cooking / to cook ) food from my home country.
4. They need ( finding / to find ) a cheaper apartment.
5. My doctor suggested ( exercising / to exercise ) three times a week.
6. We hope ( saving / to save ) enough money to visit family.
7. I avoid ( drinking / to drink ) too much coffee at night.
8. The families could not afford ( staying / to stay ) in their neighborhood.
Activity 2 · Fill in the Blank
~3 min
Write the gerund or infinitive form of the verb in parentheses.
1. Many people have started AI for health questions. (use)
2. She keeps her doctor’s advice. (follow)
3. The government plans new laws about rent. (make)
4. He quit fast food after his checkup. (eat)
5. Local families could not afford the higher rent. (pay)
6. I practice English every day after work. (speak)
7. We decided more fresh food from the market. (buy)
8. The company expects more cities next year. (expand into)

Activity 3 · Finish the Sentence
~3 min
Match each beginning to the correct ending. Write the letter on the line. (The ending must use the right form — gerund or infinitive.)
1. She enjoys…
2. He decided…
3. The doctor recommended…
4. They can’t afford…
5. I keep…
6. We plan…
7. My friend hopes…
a. …to eat less sugar before his next checkup.
b. …forgetting to take my vitamins.
c. …to buy healthier food at the market.
d. …exercising at the park after dinner.
e. …to pay for the new medicine.
f. …to lose weight by swimming three times a week.
g. …walking thirty minutes every morning.
Activity 4 · Write Your Own Sentences
~6 min
Use each verb to write a sentence about your life, health, or something from tonight. Use the correct form — gerund or infinitive.
1. I enjoy…
2. I want…
3. I avoid…
4. I decided…
5. I hope…
6. I need…
If You Finish Early
Write 2 sentences about someone else — a family member, friend, or neighbor. Use a gerund in one and an infinitive in the other.
My ____________ enjoys / avoids / keeps…
My ____________ wants / needs / decided…
