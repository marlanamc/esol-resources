Resource Website Plan

A plan for populating the public resource website with practical school and community supports.

School Resource Website Population Plan

Site: https://ebhcsjobboard.web.app/

Purpose

The EBHCS bulletin board should become a practical resource hub for students and families. It should help people quickly find trustworthy help for jobs, immigration, housing, health, food, ESOL, college, money, childcare, and legal support.

The site should not just be a list of links. Each resource should answer:

  • What is this?
  • Who is it for?
  • What can the student do next?
  • Is there a phone number, address, application, or deadline?
  • Is it available in Spanish or other languages?

Population Strategy

Start with stable evergreen resources first, then add time-sensitive posts.

Evergreen Resources

These are resources that should stay useful all year:

  • city and state help pages
  • official applications
  • community organizations
  • food and housing search tools
  • legal aid organizations
  • school and adult education resources

Time-Sensitive Posts

These are announcements that need dates and expiration review:

  • job fairs
  • training programs
  • scholarship deadlines
  • immigration clinics
  • health events
  • food distribution events
  • school registration dates

The current site already has strong categories:

  • Immigration

Start by adding 5-8 high-quality evergreen resources per category. After that, add new announcements as they come in.

Recommended Resource Writing Style

Use plain, direct language.

Good

Free help for Boston renters. Use this if you got a Notice to Quit, have a landlord problem, need legal help, or need help staying in your apartment.

Avoid

This resource provides comprehensive housing stabilization pathways for residents experiencing housing insecurity.

Students should know immediately whether the resource is for them.

Review Process

Resources can go stale quickly, especially events and training programs.

Evergreen Review

  • Check every 6 months.
  • Confirm links still work.
  • Confirm phone numbers and addresses.
  • Confirm eligibility and language access.

Time-Sensitive Review

  • Add an expiration date.
  • Remove or archive after the deadline.
  • If a program repeats, update the next cycle date.

Summer Work

  • [ ] Decide whether resources will be entered manually through the Advisor Portal or imported in bulk.
  • [ ] Add 5-8 evergreen resources per category.
  • [ ] Create a short style guide for resource descriptions.
  • [ ] Add Spanish summaries for the highest-priority resources.
  • [ ] Add expiration dates to all events, deadlines, trainings, and clinics.
  • [ ] Ask advisors which resources students request most often.
  • [ ] Create a monthly review habit for stale posts.

First Build Goal

Before the school year starts, the site should have:

  • 50 reliable evergreen resources
  • 10 current time-sensitive opportunities
  • at least 5 resources per major category
  • clear Spanish-friendly descriptions for the most important resources
  • expiration dates for anything with a deadline
  • a simple process for advisors to submit new resources
