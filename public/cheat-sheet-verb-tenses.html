Verb Tenses — Affirmative

Past Present Future
Simple

I studied English last year.

Subject + verb-ed (or irregular past)

I study English every day.

Subject + base verb (+ -s for he/she/it)

I will study English next year.

Subject + will + base verb
Continuous

I was studying when she called.

Subject + was/were + verb-ing

I am studying right now.

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

I will be studying all night.

Subject + will be + verb-ing
Perfect

I had studied before the test.

I have studied at this school.

Subject + have/has + past participle

I will have studied for 5 years by June.

Subject + will have + past participle
