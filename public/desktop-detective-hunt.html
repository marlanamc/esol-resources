Class Companion · FY27

Desktop Detective Hunt

Work as a team to explore the Class Companion dashboard. Every clue sends you to a real screen — look, click, and write the answer together.

!

How to play: Sit with your group. One person drives the mouse, everyone talks. Read each clue out loud, find the answer on the desktop dashboard, then agree on what to write. Take turns driving for each clue.

Calendar 1

School or no school?

Open Calendar. Find Thursday, September 24, 2026. Is there class that day?

Calendar 2

Name the holiday

Still in Calendar, look at October 12, 2026. What is this day called?

Settings 3

Big words, small words

Find Text Size. Switch it to Large. Read one sentence out loud together. Then switch it back to Normal.

What changed on screen?
Leaderboard 4

Who is on top?

Open Leaderboard in the top menu. Write the name in 1st place this week.

Profile 5

Count the streak

Go to your Profile. Find your streak card. How many days in a row have you practiced?

days
Home 6

This week's list

On the Home dashboard, find Weekly Checklist. How many activities are on the list this week?

Map 7

Name Week 1

Open the Map. Find Week 1. What is Week 1 called?

Points 8

Add it up

Back on your Profile card, find the number of pts next to your streak. Write your team's total points this week (add every member's points).

pts
Class resources 9

Going to miss class?

Find Class resources on your Home dashboard. What do you click if you know you are going to be absent?

Bonus · talk it out

Why keep a streak?

Talk with your group: why do you think Class Companion tracks a daily streak? Write one sentence you all agree on.
