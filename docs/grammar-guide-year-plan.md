# Grammar Guide Year Plan — Level 3 ESOL

One grammar guide per week, 34 weeks. Every guide follows the new spec: 4–5 sections, real-world theme, timeline diagram where relevant, 10-question mini quiz.

---

## The Perfect Tense Arc

| Week | Guide | What students see |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| W1 | Welcome Back | V3 preview — just noticing have/has + V3 in real sentences |
| W5 | Just, Already, Yet | Meaning only. No conjugation. "I've already eaten." Recognition. |
| W9 | Have You Ever...? | Experience meaning. "Did you go?" vs "Have you been?" |
| W13 | How Long + For / Since | Duration. "I have lived here for 3 years / since 2020." |
| W20 | Past Perfect | Full guide — arrives after 4 rounds of exposure |
| W24 | Present Perfect + PPC | Feels like a variation, not a new system |

## The Conditional Arc

| Week | Guide | What students see |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| W12 | Zero + First Conditional | Facts + real future predictions — both in one guide |
| W22 | Second Conditional | Hypothetical / unlikely. "If I had a car, I would..." |
| W31 | Third Conditional | Past regret. "If I had studied, I would have passed." |
| W34 | All Four Together | Side-by-side comparison — when to use each one |

## The Gerunds + Infinitives Arc

| Week | Guide | What students see |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| W10 | Gerunds after prepositions | interested *in*, good *at*, tired *of* — the easiest entry point |
| W18 | Infinitives after common verbs | want *to*, need *to*, hope *to*, plan *to*, would like *to* |
| W26 | Gerunds vs. infinitives | enjoy *doing* vs. want *to do* — same verb, different form |
| W29 | Meaning-change pairs | stop *to do* vs. stop *doing* — the hard stuff, with lots of practice |
| W35 | Full review | All patterns together in one real-world scenario guide |

## Notes

- **Guides marked ✅** are already built.
- **Unit 4 has 3 weeks** (W13–W15) — December is shorter.
- **Passive voice is one week only** (W27, career context) — the most useful real-world register for this level.
- **Gerunds + infinitives gets 5 touchpoints** — the most of any grammar topic, because it earns it.
- **Every guide should feel completable in 5–8 minutes on a phone.** That is the primary success metric.
