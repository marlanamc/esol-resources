## 1. The Pipe Burst

> 🖼 **Scene:** East Boston apartment, third floor. Tuesday night, 11:45 PM.  
> _Photo shows: Apartment hallway at night, dim lighting, water stain on ceiling._

- **Lucia:** I heard a noise from the ceiling last night. I looked up and saw a crack.
- **Landlord:** What did you see?
- **Lucia:** Water. It came through the ceiling above the kitchen. I grabbed a bucket and I called you right away.

Past Simple = finished actions told in order. Each one happened and ended before the next one started.

## 2. What Were You Doing?

> 🖼 **Scene:** Lucia's apartment kitchen. Same night, before the pipe burst.  
> _Photo shows: Apartment kitchen at night, rice cooker on counter, warm light._

- **Landlord:** Was anyone else awake when it happened?
- **Lucia:** My younger kids were sleeping. I was mopping the kitchen when the water started coming through.
- **Landlord:** Was your older daughter in the kitchen too?
- **Lucia:** Yes. We were getting ready for a family dinner. She was writing place cards at the table.

Past Continuous = an action already in progress at a moment in the past. It was running in the background. Form: was / were + verb-ing.

On the timeline, past continuous is a line. The pipe burst (past simple) is the dot that interrupted it.

**Exercise: Practice: Past Continuous**
- The kids ___ when the pipe burst. Which form fits? _(options: slept (past simple) / were sleeping (past continuous) / sleep (present simple))_
- "She was mop the floor when the water came through." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. Should be 'was mopping'.)_
- Unscramble:
- Her daughter ___ (write) names on envelopes when the pipe burst.

## 3. When and While

- **Amina:** Lucia, what happened last night? I heard noise from upstairs.
- **Lucia:** I was mopping the kitchen when the pipe burst. Water came everywhere.
- **Amina:** While you were calling the landlord, I heard you on the phone. I almost knocked.
- **Lucia:** I wish you had! The kids woke up while I was moving furniture away from the water.

Use when + past simple for the interruption. Use while + past continuous for the action that was in progress.

Quick tip: the continuous action is the background (line). The simple action is the event that cut into it or happened during it (dot).

**Exercise: Practice: When and While**
- She ___ the floor when the pipe burst. Which form fits in the blank? _(options: mopped / was mopping)_
- The kids woke up ___ Lucia was moving the furniture. _(options: when / while)_
- "While the pipe burst, she was mopping." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. 'While' goes with the continuous action. Should be: 'While she was mopping, the pipe burst.')_
- Unscramble:
- The kids ___ (sleep) while the water came through the ceiling.

## 4. Telling the Landlord

> 🖼 **Scene:** Lucia's apartment. Wednesday morning, 8:00 AM.  
> _Photo shows: A woman looking at her smartphone on a couch._

- **Brian:** Lucia, this is Brian from the building. I heard about the pipe. Can you walk me through what happened?
- **Lucia:** Yes. It was almost midnight. The kids were sleeping. I was mopping the kitchen when I heard a crack above me.
- **Brian:** And then?
- **Lucia:** Water came down fast. I grabbed a bucket. While I was holding the bucket, I called the landlord with my other hand.
- **Brian:** Okay, I have enough to file the report. Thank you.

Lucia uses both tenses together: past simple for the sequence of events, past continuous for what was already happening in the background. Choose the right tense for each blank below.

**Exercise: Practice: Choose the Right Tense**
- The kids ___ when the pipe burst. (They were already in bed.) _(options: slept (past simple) / were sleeping (past continuous))_
- Lucia ___ a bucket right away. (One quick action.) _(options: grabbed (past simple) / was grabbing (past continuous))_
- While she ___ the bucket, she called the landlord. _(options: held (past simple) / was holding (past continuous))_
- "Brian called Lucia while she was sleeping." Which action was already in progress? _(options: Brian calling / Lucia sleeping / Both were in progress.)_
- Water ___ (come) down fast when Lucia grabbed a bucket.
