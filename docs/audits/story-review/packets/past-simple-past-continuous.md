## 1. Past Simple: What Happened

> 🖼 **Scene:** Meridian Street, East Boston. Tuesday, 7:40 AM. The 116 just left.  
> _Photo shows: A street filled with parked cars next to tall buildings._

Fred missed the 116 bus this morning. At class that evening, he tells his classmate Amara what happened.

- **Fred:** I missed the 116 this morning! I walked to the Blue Line and arrived at work twenty minutes late.
- **Amara:** Oh no! How did you miss it?
- **Fred:** The bus left one minute early. I ran to the corner but it was already gone.

Past Simple = finished actions, one at a time. Each one happened and ended. We tell them in order.

Notice: each action is a single dot on the timeline. They happened, they finished, we move on.

- "She arrive late to class yesterday." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. Should be 'arrived')_
- Unscramble to make a past simple sentence:
- The bus ___ (leave) one minute early.

## 2. Past Continuous: What Was Already in Progress

> 🖼 **Scene:** This morning, Blue Line station. Fred walked here after he missed the bus.  
> _Photo shows: photo of train station_

- **Amara:** So what were you doing when the bus left?
- **Fred:** I was crossing Meridian. I was listening to an English podcast and thinking about work. I didn't hear it.
- **Amara:** Did you try to run after it?
- **Fred:** I tried! But I was carrying a hot coffee. I couldn't run fast.

Past Continuous = an action that was already in progress at a moment in the past. It was running in the background. It is not finished yet when we describe it.

Form: was / were + verb-ing

On the timeline, past continuous is a line, not a dot. It was already going when the moment arrived.

**Exercise: Practice: Past Continuous**
- Fred ___ to an English podcast when the bus left. Which form fits? _(options: listened (past simple) / was listening (past continuous) / listens (present simple))_
- "They were wait at the bus stop." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. Should be 'were waiting')_
- Unscramble:
- He ___ (think) about work when the bus came.

## 3. When + While: Putting Them Together

Use when + past simple for the interruption. Use while + past continuous for the action in progress.

Quick tip: the continuous action (line) is the background. The simple action (dot) is the event that happened inside it or interrupted it.

**Exercise: Practice: When and While**
- Fred ___ to an English podcast when the bus came. Which form fits in the blank? _(options: listened / was listening)_
- The bus left while he ___ the street. Which fits? _(options: crossed / was crossing)_
- "She was talking on the phone when she was dropping her bag." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. 'dropped' fits the interruption, not 'was dropping')_
- Unscramble:
- He ___ (carry) a hot coffee when he missed the bus.

## 4. Tell the Story

> 🖼 **Scene:** East Boston school entrance. Wednesday, 2:45 PM.  
> _Photo shows: A group of people walking down a sidewalk._

- **Elena:** Yesterday was crazy. I was working a double shift when my son's school called.
- **Fabienne:** What happened? Is he okay?
- **Elena:** Yes, he fell at recess. While I was driving to school, it started to rain. I got there in ten minutes.
- **Fabienne:** In the rain? Were the roads okay? Did someone cover for you at work?
- **Elena:** My manager did. First time ever!

Read Elena's story again. Choose past simple or past continuous for each blank.

**Exercise: Practice: Tell the Story**
- Elena ___ a double shift when the school called. _(options: worked (past simple) / was working (past continuous))_
- Her son ___ at recess. It was a finished event. _(options: fell (past simple) / was falling (past continuous))_
- While she ___ to school, it started to rain. _(options: drove (past simple) / was driving (past continuous))_
- "While her son was waiting at school, Elena was arrived." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. 'arrived' is past simple, not 'was arrived')_
- Unscramble Elena's sentence:
- It ___ (start) to rain while she was driving.
