## 1. Where Are You Going?

> 🖼 **Scene:** Maverick Blue Line Station, East Boston. Tuesday, 7 PM.  
> _Photo shows: Woman standing outside a subway station entrance at dusk, looking at her phone._

Amara just got off the train. Hannah from the community center told her to walk up Meridian Street.

- **Amara:** Jean, I just got off the train at Maverick. How do I get to the community center?
- **Jean:** Walk straight on Maverick Square. Turn left on Meridian Street.
- **Amara:** Then what?
- **Jean:** Cross the street at the light. Go two more blocks. You will see it on the right.

Imperatives = commands and instructions. Use the base form of the verb. There is no subject. you are talking directly to the person.

No subject needed. You do not say "You turn left." Just say "Turn left." The verb is first.

## 2. How to Say "Don't"

> 🖼 **Scene:** East Boston Community Center, bulletin board near the entrance.  
> _Photo shows: Close-up of handwritten notes and papers pinned to a community bulletin board._

A volunteer posted directions and warnings on the board. Read what she wrote.

Notice from Claudette, Volunteer Coordinator

Walk to the end of the hallway and turn right for Room 4.

Don't turn right at the front door. that leads to the storage room.

Don't use the side entrance after 8 PM. It locks automatically.

Please ask at the front desk if you need help.

Please sign in before class. Thank you!

Negative imperative: Don't + base verb. Use it to tell someone NOT to do something. Polite imperative: Please + base verb. Same structure, softer tone.

**Exercise: Don't or please?**
- Claudette wants to warn students NOT to do something. Which sentence does she write? _(options: Park in front of the gate. / Don't park in front of the gate. / Not park in front of the gate.)_
- Claudette wants to give a polite instruction to sign in. Which is best? _(options: You should turn off your phone in class. / Don't turn off your phone in class. / Please turn off your phone in class.)_

**Exercise: Check the sign**
- "Please asks at the front desk if you need help." Is this correct? _(options: Correct / Not correct. Should be "Please ask" (base verb, no -s))_

**Exercise: Write the warning**
- ___ use the side entrance after 8 PM.

## 3. Where Is It?

> 🖼 **Scene:** Meridian Street, East Boston. A sunny afternoon.  
> _Photo shows: Two neighbors talking on a city sidewalk with storefronts in the background._

- **Carlos:** Excuse me, I'm looking for the health clinic on Meridian Street. Do you know where it is?
- **Linh:** Yes! It's next to the pharmacy, across from the laundromat.
- **Carlos:** Is it the big building?
- **Linh:** Right. It's between the pharmacy and the bakery, on the corner of Meridian and Maverick.

Prepositions of location tell you WHERE something is in relation to something else.

**Exercise: Choose the preposition**

## 4. Step by Step

> 🖼 **Scene:** Bennington Street bus stop, East Boston. Saturday morning.  
> _Photo shows: A city bus stop on a busy urban street with pedestrians walking past._

Elena is writing directions for her cousin Miguel. He just got off the bus on Bennington Street and needs to walk to the public library.

Elena's directions for Miguel

First, walk straight on Bennington Street for two blocks.

Then, turn left on Bremen Street.

Next, go past the park. The library is on the right, next to the community garden.

Finally, cross at the crosswalk and walk in front of the big blue doors.

That same week, Elena texted Miguel directions to the flower market on Chelsea Street: Turn right after the bus stop. Walk past the bakery. The flowers are next to the entrance.

Use First, Then, Next, Finally to put directions in order. Each step starts with an imperative + preposition to say what to do AND where.

**Exercise: Elena's directions**
- Unscramble Elena's first direction:
- Miguel finds the library. It is "___ the right, next to the community garden." Which word fits? _(options: on / in / at)_
- Elena uses four steps. She writes: First... Then... ___... Finally. Which word goes in the blank? _(options: After / Next / Soon)_

**Exercise: Write the step**
- Elena's second step: Then, ___ left on Bremen Street.
