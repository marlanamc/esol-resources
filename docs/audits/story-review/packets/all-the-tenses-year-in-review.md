## 1. One Year, Eleven Tenses

> 🖼 **Scene:** East Boston evening ESOL class. Thursday, June 5, 7 PM.  
> _Photo shows: Adult students seated at desks in an evening classroom for the last class of the year._

Ms. Tran stands at the front of the room. It is the last class of the year. She asks the class to look back at September and ahead to fall.

- **Ms. Tran:** You started this class in September. You have been studying all year. What will you do next?
- **Rosa:** I will enroll in Level 4 in September. I just need to make sure it fits around my lunch shifts.
- **Rosa:** I already work Saturdays at the hotel, too. Two evenings a week is about all I can manage.
- **David:** Me too. By August I will have finished Level 3. I hope we can stay in the same class.

Eleven tenses, one timeline. You used all of these in real life this year.

Simple = fact or finished. Continuous = in progress. Perfect = connected to a reference point. Past perfect = before another past event. Future perfect = done by a future date.

**Exercise: Which sentence fits?**
- Rosa's schedule: she goes to class every Tuesday and Thursday. _(options: She is taking class every Tuesday and Thursday. / She takes class every Tuesday and Thursday. / She took class every Tuesday and Thursday.)_
- Right now, Rosa is in class and her phone is on silent. _(options: She sits in class and her phone is on silent. / She is sitting in class and her phone is on silent. / She has sat in class and her phone is on silent.)_
- By August, Rosa ___ (finish) Level 3.

## 2. Every Tuesday vs. Right Now

> 🖼 **Scene:** Taqueria on Meridian Street. Wednesday, 1:30 PM.  
> _Photo shows: A busy restaurant dining room during the lunch rush with staff moving between tables._

Rosa works lunch shifts at a restaurant on Meridian Street. Hotel laundry on Saturdays. ESOL class on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. Supervisor Jennifer covers the register when Rosa leaves at 3.

- **Jennifer:** You usually leave at three, right? Do you need to go early today?
- **Rosa:** Yes, if that’s OK. My daughter has a field trip tomorrow, and I need to pick up a few things. I am texting the sitter now.
- **Jennifer:** Sure. I usually cover the register for you on Wednesdays anyway. What time do you need to leave?
- **Rosa:** Two thirty, please. Thanks. I know we are running behind today.

Present Simple = routines and facts. Present Continuous = happening right now or this week.

- Hold on. Rosa ___ the sitter right now. (text) _(options: texts / is texting / has texted)_
- Jennifer usually ___ the register when Rosa leaves. (cover)

## 3. What Happened, What Was Already Done

> 🖼 **Scene:** Rosa's apartment, Orient Heights. January 14, 11:45 PM.  
> _Photo shows: A person cooking at a stove in a small apartment kitchen at night._

Rosa tells her landlord what happened in January: what she was doing, what happened next, and what she had already done before calling.

- **Rosa:** I was cooking when water came through the ceiling.
- **Landlord:** That was when you called me, just before midnight?
- **Rosa:** Yes. I had already put a bucket under the leak, but it filled up fast.
- **Rosa:** Then I turned off the stove and moved everything away from the water.

Past Simple = finished event. Past Continuous = in progress when something else happened. Past Perfect = already finished before another past event.

**Exercise: Pick the right past form**
- Water ___ through the ceiling while Rosa was cooking. (come) _(options: came / was coming / had come)_
- Rosa ___ dinner when the pipe burst. (cook) _(options: cooked / was cooking / had cooked)_
- She ___ (already save) for the bus pass before she applied.

**Exercise: Find the error**
- "I was cooking when water was coming through the ceiling." _(options: Correct as written / Should be 'came' (past simple for the burst) / Should be 'had come' (past perfect))_

## 4. From September Until Now

> 🖼 **Scene:** Hotel laundry room after a double shift. Wednesday, 9:30 PM.  
> _Photo shows: A woman on a video call on her phone after a long work shift, looking tired._

Rosa calls her sister Teresa after a long restaurant shift. Teresa is at home waiting for her.

- **Teresa:** Still at work? I haven’t seen you all week.
- **Rosa:** Just finished. I have worked here for three years, and this has been my busiest week yet.
- **Rosa:** And I have been taking two buses since the car broke down in March. It takes forever to get home.
- **Teresa:** That sounds exhausting. I have made dinner, so you won’t need to cook tonight.

Present Perfect = past connected to now (experience, change, duration with for/since). Present Perfect Continuous = ongoing action from past until now, often with for/since.

## 5. What's Next: All Four Future Tenses

> 🖼 **Scene:** 121 bus toward Maverick Square. Thursday, 9:15 PM.  
> _Photo shows: Passengers seated inside a city bus at dusk heading home after work._

Before the last class, Rosa and classmate David talk on the 121 bus about finishing Level 3 and their plans for September.

- **David:** Level 4 in the fall?
- **Rosa:** That’s the plan. I will be taking evening classes while I keep working lunch shifts.
- **Rosa:** By August I will have finished Level 3. It feels good to be moving on.
- **David:** It does. By September I will have been studying here for a full year. I will ask Ms. Tran about the Level 4 schedule tonight.

Future Simple = plan or prediction. Future Continuous = in progress at a future time. Future Perfect = done by a future date. Future Perfect Continuous = duration by a future date.

Rosa looks out the window and thinks about her year. She was tired when class started in September. She has been studying all year. She takes this bus every Thursday. Tonight she is sitting next to a woman who has lived here since 2019.

**Exercise: Pick the future form**
- Rosa's plan: she ___ in Level 4 in September. (enroll) _(options: will enroll / will be enrolling / will have enrolled)_
- In September she ___ class twice a week while she still works. (take) _(options: will take / will be taking / will have taken)_
- By August she ___ (finish) Level 3.

**Exercise: Future perfect continuous**
- By the last class tonight, Rosa ___ evening ESOL for a full year. (attend) _(options: will attend / will be attending / will have been attending)_
- By tonight I ___ (attend) evening ESOL for a full year.

**Exercise: Find the error**
- "By August I will finish Level 3." Time word: By August. _(options: Correct as written / Should be 'will have finished' / Should be 'will be finishing')_
