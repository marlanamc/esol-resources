- **Slug:** `verb-forms-overview`

### Scenarios

**Five codes, every week** — Every week you will take a verb quiz in this app. Each quiz asks about five forms of two verbs. Learn the codes now so the quizzes feel familiar, not scary.

**V1, V1-3rd, V1-ing: forms you use now** — V1 = base form. V1-3rd = he/she/it form (usually add -s). V1-ing = base + -ing for actions in progress.

**V2 and V3: the past forms** — V2 = finished past action. V3 = the form after have / has / had. You will use V3 a lot later in the year.

**be and have: the tricky ones** — Your first quiz focuses on be and have. These verbs do not follow the regular pattern. Every form is different.

**Ready for Verb Quiz 1** — You do not need to memorize everything today. This guide gives you the map. The quiz helps you practice two verbs at a time.

**Present Simple: every day, every week** — Present Simple = routines, schedules, and facts that stay true over time.
