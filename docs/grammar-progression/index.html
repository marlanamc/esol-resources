One required grammar guide each week, with short returns to earlier learning. The revised sequence now teaches past perfect continuous, future perfect, and future perfect continuous.

How the year is built

  • One required guide alongside required practice; additional reference guides are optional.
  • New lessons stay focused on one useful meaning and pattern. Each week includes an “I can” goal and a “Bring back” reminder.
  • Quiz grammar generally follows the required guide. W4 includes foundations, W15 is a cumulative recap, and final bonus quizzes may include previously taught review.
  • W22 and W30 introduce a small core lesson and protect time for catch-up. They are not review-only weeks.
  • Meaningful attempts and revisions earn effort credit. Accuracy remains separate from participation, and duplicate technical requests must not duplicate awards.

What changed

  • W21: used to in past work routines; workplace rules become brief review.
  • W23: enjoy doing / want to do; phrasal verbs remain vocabulary practice and optional reference.
  • W26: past perfect continuous, building on W25.
  • W32: future perfect through small goals and deadlines.
  • W35: future perfect continuous through year-end progress; full gerund review stays optional.
  • W36: review all taught forms. Only future continuous remains an optional preview.
