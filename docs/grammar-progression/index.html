What each week includes

  • One required grammar guide, an “I can” goal, and a short reminder to bring back earlier learning.
  • From Week 6 onward, exactly six required adult vocabulary words or phrases, with Word Lab listening and pronunciation practice. There are no optional extra vocabulary sets.
  • Recommended Grammar Companion pages, linked directly to the relevant page, with the specific sections students should review. Partial matches are clearly labeled.
  • Vocabulary fill-in-the-blank previews with complete word-or-phrase answers, meaning clues, and distinct answer choices. Open “Review vocabulary fill-in-the-blank questions” within a weekly plan.
  • Lesson and quiz previews, including teacher answer keys, plus optional in-person games where they fit the week.

Keep the level useful and manageable

Weeks 1–5 remain as already taught. Weeks 6–36 build adult fluency through language such as raise a concern, security deposit, reimburse, and cut back on. Familiar words support the examples without taking up every core slot.

Weeks 15 and 30 deliberately reuse previously taught vocabulary. Weeks 22 and 30 protect catch-up time alongside a short core grammar lesson. Optional classroom games replace another class activity; they are not extra required homework.

Recognize meaningful attempts and revisions with effort credit. Record in-person participation through the teacher points workflow. This local reference is for planning and does not save student answers or award points.

The revised grammar path

  • W21: used to for past work routines.
  • W23: enjoy doing / want to do.
  • W26: past perfect continuous, building on earlier perfect and duration work.
  • W32: future perfect through goals and deadlines.
  • W35: future perfect continuous through year-end progress.
  • W36: review taught forms; future continuous remains a preview.
