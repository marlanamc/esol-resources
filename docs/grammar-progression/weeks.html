Week by week

Each week at a glance

The topic, required grammar guide, what students do in the app, the vocab set, the verbs of the week, and a couple of ideas to try in class.

App details come from the course map, the weekly vocab list and the verb quiz plan as of October 18, 2026. "Ideas to try in class" are suggestions and are not in the app. Activities marked planned, not built yet are on the course map, but nothing is behind them yet: students see them greyed out with a "soon" label. Dashed verb chips are review verbs from an earlier quiz.
