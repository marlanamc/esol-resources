# Grammar guide cast sheet

Students meet the same few people all year, so those people need to stay the same people. This sheet fixes who they are.

**Core cast:** the names below are reserved. Whenever one of them appears in a guide (dialogue, caption, exercise or quiz), the guide must fit their bio. Their lives can move forward over the year as listed under "Arc", but nothing else should change. If a story needs a different job or family, give that character a different name.

**Everyone else is free.** Other names, including the common American names used for supervisors, HR, clerks and landlords (Jennifer, Kevin, Mark, Scott, Sarah, Brian...), can be a different person in every guide. Inside one guide, a name is always one person.

Before adding a core character to a guide, check their bio here. When a guide moves a core character's life forward, add it to their Arc.

## Rosa: the main recurring character

- **From:** Mexico (Mexican American). Speaks Spanish.
- **Work:** hotel housekeeper at an airport hotel, Monday to Friday, 7 to 3. Has worked in housekeeping since 2023 and lived in East Boston since 2021.
- **Family:** a school-age daughter. Her sister Teresa helps with the kids. Cousin Javier.
- **Home:** rents an apartment on Meridian Street. Amara is her neighbor in the same building, and Diego is a neighbor nearby.
- **Getting around:** no car. Takes the 116 bus.
- **Class:** evening ESOL, Tuesday and Thursday, with Ms. Tran.
- **Arc:** fall: the bus detour week (W6). Winter: the heat breaks and she learns her tenant rights (W17), then her lease ends and she has to find a new place (W16–18). Spring: she interviews for a better housekeeping job at a hotel on Meridian Street and gets it (W24). June: looks back on the year (W36).
