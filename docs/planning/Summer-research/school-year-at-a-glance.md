# School Year At A Glance

A basic map of the ESOL Level 3 year — same shape as the [September Unit 1 Revised Split](september-app-onboarding-plan.md#september-unit-1-revised-split), scaled to all ten units.

**Class schedule:** Tuesday + Thursday  
**Weekly rhythm:** Tuesday = introduce · Thursday = review + **weekly app quiz**  
**Assessment:** Pre-test (September) · weekly app quizzes all year · post-test (June) — **no unit quizzes, no final presentations**  
**App spine:** This Week's Path every week (see [Absent Student Catch-Up Plan](absent-student-catch-up-plan.md))

## Calendar Notes

| When | What to plan for |
|---|---|
| **December** | ~**2 weeks** of class only (short month — compress Unit 4) |
| **June** | ~**2 weeks** of class only (short month — post-test + end-of-year **party**, not presentations) |
| **February** | **1 week off** (no Tue/Thu classes — app catch-up path still open) |
| **April** | **1 week off** (no Tue/Thu classes — app catch-up path still open) |

Everything else below assumes a normal **4 teaching weeks** per month (~8 class days).

## Month-By-Month Splits

### September — Unit 1: Getting to Know You

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Chromebook + login + scavenger hunt + class routines + **pre-test window** | None or parts of speech preview only |
| Week 2 | App habit + first weekly path + **English vowel names** (A, E, I focus) | Parts of speech (short) |
| Week 3 | Getting to know you + **verb forms overview** + **-ed endings intro** (-t, -d, -id) | Present simple review + verb / -ed charts on warm-up back |
| Week 4 | Personal daily life + **present perfect intro** + weekly **-ed** practice with 3 verbs | Present perfect (short intro — life experience frames) |

Detail: [September App Onboarding Plan](september-app-onboarding-plan.md)

### October — Unit 2: Daily Life in the Community

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Community places + daily routines | Information questions |
| Week 2 | Transportation + maps/directions | Imperatives + directions language |
| Week 3 | Digital literacy Layer 2 (scams, forms, email safety) | Modals: can / should / must (light) |
| Week 4 | Staying connected to family + phone English | Present perfect spiral (frames from September) |

### November — Unit 3: Community Participation

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Helping, volunteering, joining groups | Gerunds after prepositions (light) |
| Week 2 | Civic participation + public meetings | Imperatives + suggestions |
| Week 3 | Voting, ballots, contacting officials | Reading focus — claims and reasons |
| Week 4 | Case study: a community issue | Zero / first conditionals (light) |

### December — Unit 4: Consumer Smarts *(~2 weeks only)*

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Smart spending + compare prices + **numbers (through trillions)** | Comparatives + superlatives |
| Week 2 | Bills, fees, returns, scams — read big numbers in context | Quantifiers + review |

Use December's short month for **consumer math language**: million, billion, trillion on price tags, bills, news headlines, and loan/fee examples. Skip a full 4-week spread — highest-value consumer tasks first.

### January — Unit 5: Housing

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Housing basics + classroom language refresh | Parts of speech |
| Week 2 | Describing rooms + comparing options | Superlatives + quantifiers |
| Week 3 | Tenant–landlord calls + repair requests | Information questions |
| Week 4 | Housing problems + solutions | Past simple vs past continuous review |

### February — Unit 6: Workforce Preparation *(vacation week)*

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Resume + workplace basics | Zero + first conditionals |
| Week 2 | Work stories + schedules | Past perfect (steps 1–4) |
| — | **Vacation week — no class** | App catch-up only |
| Week 3 | Workplace rights + obligation language | Modals: obligation + permission |

After vacation: pick up with Week 3 content — do not assign a backlog of missed weeks.

### March — Unit 7: Career Awareness

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Job applications + interviews | Gerunds after prepositions |
| Week 2 | Career stories + work experience | **Present perfect + present perfect continuous (together)** |
| Week 3 | Workplace advocacy + negotiation | **Present perfect + PPC spiral** (same pair — more speaking) |
| Week 4 | Pay stubs, wage math, agency navigation | Infinitives vs gerunds |

**Teaching note:** Teach present perfect and present perfect continuous **as a pair** here — not separately across different months. Career stories (*I have worked…* / *I have been working…*) make the contrast natural. September intro and October spiral stay light; this is the **in-depth** block.

### April — Unit 8: Health *(vacation week)*

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |

| Week 2 | Clinic visits + symptoms | Advice modals (review) |
| — | **Vacation week — no class** | App catch-up only |

May opens with reported speech if April runs short.

### May — Unit 9: Holistic Wellness

| Week | Focus | Grammar (light) |

## Assessment Rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| September (Week 1–2) | **Pre-test** (program requirement — schedule alongside boot camp) |
| Every Thursday | **Weekly app quiz** (topic + grammar + vocab from the week) |
| June (Week 1) | **Post-test** (program requirement — before the end-of-year party) |

**No separate unit quizzes.** Monthly units change the topic; the quiz format stays the same every week on the app.

**No final presentations.** The last June class is an **end-of-year party**.

## Present Perfect Sequence

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Light intro** | September Week 4 | Life experience frames only — *I have lived…*, *Have you ever…?* (no PPC) |
| **Spiral** | October Week 4 | Reuse September frames in family/community speaking |
| **In depth (paired)** | **March Unit 7, Weeks 2–3** | **Present perfect + present perfect continuous together** — career stories, *How long have you been…?* / *I have been working…* |

September is not the time for PPC or PP vs past simple in depth. When the year focuses on present perfect properly, teach **perfect and perfect continuous as a pair** — students see the contrast in real work-history language.

## Pronunciation Foundations (September → All Year)

| Topic | When | What students learn |
|---|---|---|
| **English vowel names** | September Week 2 (one class) | Letter names **A, E, I, O, U** — extra focus on **I / E / A** (Spanish letter-name habits differ) |
| **-ed endings (-t, -d, -id)** | September Week 3 (brief intro) | Three ending sounds; sort the week's verbs; chart stays on warm-up back |
| **Daily spiral** | Every week after September | Thursday phonics + 3 verbs of the week on the **-ed** chart; one quiz question when relevant |

**September goal:** students know *what* the three -ed endings are and *where* to look on the chart — not a full phonics unit. Depth builds all year through repetition.

## Monthly Pronunciation Focus (Minimal Pair Lab)

Each month has **one main contrast** from the app **Minimal Pair Lab** (plus **Sentence Listening** sets for the same sound). Thursday phonics (10–15 min) + a **required app path step** — not optional browse.

**All year:** weekly **-ed** spiral on the warm-up back (September Week 3 on).

| Month | App contrast | Lab ID | In-class hook | Why this month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **September** | Vowel names + **-ed** (-t, -d, -id) | `-ed` game when ready | Spell names aloud; sort 3 verbs | Letter-name habits (I/E/A); past-tense foundation |
| **October** | **B vs V** | `b-v` | ban/van, bus, bank, very | High-value for Spanish speakers; community/transport vocab |
| **November** | **Short i vs Long e** | `short-i-long-e` | sit/seat, bit/beat in civic words | Common confusion — Spanish *i* ≈ English long *e* |
| **December** *(short)* | **Spiral: B vs V + short i/long e** | `b-v` + `short-i-long-e` | Consumer words + numbers aloud | Reinforce top two contrasts in 2 weeks |
| **January** | **R vs L** | `r-l` | room, floor, repair, landlord | Housing + phone clarity |
| **February** | **V vs W** | `v-w` | very, work, interview, went | Workforce speech after vacation week |
| **March** | **S vs Th** | `s-th` | think, thank, month, math | Career/advocacy — professional clarity |
| **April** | **Sh vs Ch** | `sh-ch` | check, chest, choose, pharmacy | Health unit vocabulary |
| **May** | **P vs B** + **B vs V refresh** | `p-b` (+ quick `b-v`) | cap/cab, pay/bay; revisit van/very | Complete the lab set; spiral priority contrast |
| **June** *(short)* | **Mixed review** | top contrasts + `-ed` | Party games — listen & point | Post-test week; celebrate progress |

### Weekly rhythm (same every month)

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Tuesday** | Mention the month's sound in warm-up ("Today we're listening for **B** vs **V**") |
| **Thursday** | 10–15 min phonics block: listen → repeat → minimal pair drill → 2–3 pairs in sentences |
| **App path** | Assign **Minimal Pair Lab** round for this month's contrast (required, due Thursday) |
| **Optional** | **Sentence Listening** set for the same contrast when students need sentence context |
| **Every week** | **-ed** chart: add 3 verbs of the week; one quiz question when relevant |

### Teacher prep (once per month)

- [ ] Note this month's contrast on the warm-up back header
- [ ] Assign the matching **Minimal Pair Lab** activity in This Week's Path
- [ ] Pick 4–6 pairs from the lab for in-class choral drill (easy → medium)
- [ ] Use the lab's **Spanish tip** line when students ask why the sounds differ

App contrasts live in `src/lib/minimal-pairs-data.ts` — seven sets: `short-i-long-e`, `b-v`, `r-l`, `sh-ch`, `s-th`, `v-w`, `p-b`.

## What Stays The Same All Year

- **This Week's Path** in the app (required vs optional labeled)
- **Missed class?** same catch-up steps every week
- **Make-up deadline:** Monday 6:00 PM
- **6 vocabulary words + 3 verbs** per week (verb quizzes use **v1 / v1-3rd / v1-ing / v2 / v3** from September Week 3 on)
- **-ed endings (-t, -d, -id)** on the warm-up back from September Week 3 on — brief daily/weekly spiral
- **Monthly pronunciation focus** — one Minimal Pair Lab contrast per month (see table above); Thursday phonics + required app step
- **Tuesday introduce / Thursday review** grammar pattern
- **Phone English** woven into every unit (not its own month)
