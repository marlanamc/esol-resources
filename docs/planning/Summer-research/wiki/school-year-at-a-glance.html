School Year At A Glance

A basic map of the ESOL Level 3 year — same shape as the September Unit 1 Revised Split, scaled to all ten units.

Class schedule: Tuesday + Thursday Weekly rhythm: Tuesday = introduce · Thursday = review + weekly app quiz Assessment: Pre-test (September) · weekly app quizzes all year · post-test (June) — no unit quizzes, no final presentations App spine: This Week's Path every week (see Absent Student Catch-Up Plan)

Calendar Notes

WhenWhat to plan for
December~2 weeks of class only (short month — compress Unit 4)
June~2 weeks of class only (short month — post-test + end-of-year party, not presentations)
February1 week off (no Tue/Thu classes — app catch-up path still open)
April1 week off (no Tue/Thu classes — app catch-up path still open)

Everything else below assumes a normal 4 teaching weeks per month (~8 class days).

Assessment Rhythm

WhenWhat
September (Week 1–2)Pre-test (program requirement — schedule alongside boot camp)
Every ThursdayWeekly app quiz (topic + grammar + vocab from the week)
June (Week 1)Post-test (program requirement — before the end-of-year party)

No separate unit quizzes. Monthly units change the topic; the quiz format stays the same every week on the app.

No final presentations. The last June class is an end-of-year party.

Present Perfect Sequence

PhaseWhenWhat
Light introSeptember Week 4Life experience frames only — I have lived…, Have you ever…? (no PPC)
SpiralOctober Week 4Reuse September frames in family/community speaking
In depth (paired)March Unit 7, Weeks 2–3Present perfect + present perfect continuous together — career stories, How long have you been…? / I have been working…

September is not the time for PPC or PP vs past simple in depth. When the year focuses on present perfect properly, teach perfect and perfect continuous as a pair — students see the contrast in real work-history language.

---

What Stays The Same All Year

  • This Week's Path in the app (required vs optional labeled)
  • Missed class? same catch-up steps every week
  • Make-up deadline: Monday 6:00 PM
  • 6 vocabulary words + 3 verbs per week (verb quizzes use v1 / v1-3rd / v1-ing / v2 / v3 from September Week 3 on)
  • Tuesday introduce / Thursday review grammar pattern
  • Phone English woven into every unit (not its own month)
