What happens in class

Tuesday: Stations practice day

We practice last Thursday's grammar. You sit at a table with a small group. Every 25 minutes, your group moves to the next table.

1Warm-up: work in your packet. Late? Start here.
2Speak: talk and practice sounds with a teacher.
3Read or Game: a short reading or a card game.
4Real World: a real paper, like a letter or a form. Read it and practice a phone call.

Thursday: New grammar learning day

  • Warm-up when you arrive. It reviews last week.
  • We learn the new grammar together, with examples and practice.
  • We answer your questions from the app.
  • At 6 PM the new quiz opens. Do it at home before next Tuesday.

Bring: your phone, a pencil, and your packet or notebook.

Every week, I…

study the Words before Tuesday.
do the Weekly Quiz on Thursday or Friday (due Tuesday 6 PM). MUST DO
look through the Grammar guide before Thursday.
practice English at home and tap + Log in the app.

I missed class. What do I do?

Open the app → Map → find the week → follow the steps.

Always do the quiz, even if you were absent.

I am new!

Start with this week. You do not need to do old weeks or old quizzes.

Questions? Ask a teacher or a classmate. We will help you!
