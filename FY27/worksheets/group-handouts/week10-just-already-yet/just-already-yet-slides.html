The big idea

Where is it on the time line?

All three use have / has + V3.

Schedule card · top

Where does the word go?

How it works

  1. Find the time.
  2. Draw a NOW line on the time line.
  3. Before NOW = done → already or just.
  4. After NOW = not done → yet.

Worksheet · Part C

Fix the mistake.

Each sentence has 1 mistake. Find it with a partner.

  1. Kevin has eaten already breakfast.Kevin has already eaten breakfast.ALREADY goes after has, before V3.
  2. Mandy just has bought the turkey.Mandy has just bought the turkey.has comes first, then JUST.
  3. Mandy has cleaned the house yet.Mandy has already cleaned the house.No YET in a + sentence. (Or the question: Has Mandy cleaned the house yet?)
  4. Kevin hasn't went to bed yet.Kevin hasn't gone to bed yet.went is V2. We need V3: gone.
  5. Have you finish your holiday shopping yet?Have you finished your holiday shopping yet?have + V3: finished.
