don't have to
Not needed. You choose. It's OK to do it, and OK not to do it.
I don't have to work on Sunday.
She doesn't have to pay for the bus.
he / she / it: doesn't have to

NO CHOICE
must
A strong rule. Often the law or a safety rule.
You must wear a seatbelt.
Workers must wash their hands.
Signs and forms use must.

You can't park here.
must not ≠ don't have to
must not = NO! · don't have to = you choose
