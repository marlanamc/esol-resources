The Choice Meter · Teacher Page

Print the 3 student sheets landscape, one-sided, in color. Sheets 1–2 are the accordion; sheet 3 is the cards (one cutter pass: 3 cuts each way).

Put it together (about 5 minutes)

  1. Lay sheet 1 and sheet 2 face up, side by side. Put tape on the back where they meet. The panels go cover, can, may, don't have to, should, have to, must, must not.
  2. Fold on every dashed line, zigzag (in, out, in, out). It stands up like a fence.
  3. Cut out the 16 cards. Write a name or initials on the back.

Games

  1. Sort it. Stand the book up. Put each card in front of a panel and say the sentence: You must wear a seatbelt. Pick a subject for each card: I, you, she…
  2. Argue it. Partners compare. Different panel? Explain why. Some cards fit two panels (see the right column), and that's good talk.
  3. Blank cards. Each student writes a rule from their own life: work, home, the clinic, their country. The partner sorts it.
  4. Draw the sign. On each panel, draw a real sign: No Smoking, Parking, Wash Your Hands…
