Desktop Detective Hunt

My ESOL App — look around the app with your team.

Name:  
How to play: Sit with your group. Read each clue out loud, find the answer in the app, then agree on what to write. Take turns using the mouse for each clue.
  1. Calendar

    Open Calendar. Find Thursday, September 24, 2026. Is there class that day?

    YES / NO — because it says:
  2. Calendar

    Still in Calendar, look at December. What is our last day of class in December?

  3. Settings

    Find Text Size and try both Large and Normal. Which size do you prefer?

  4. Leaderboard

    Open Leaderboard in the top menu. Write the name in 1st place this week.

  5. Profile

    Go to your Profile. Find your streak card. How many days in a row have you practiced?

  6. Home

    On the Home screen, find Weekly Checklist. Click "Show full week." How many activities are on the list this week?

  7. Map

    Open the Map. Find Week 1. What is Week 1 called?

  8. Map

    Still on the Map, find Optional Practice. How many items are in Optional Practice?

  9. Class resources

    Find Class resources on your Home screen. What do you click if you know you are going to be absent?

  10. Search

    Use the search bar and type "games." Write the name of the first result that shows up.

  11. Install the app

    Did you install the app on your phone's home screen? If not, ask someone in your group to help you.

Bonus — talk it out

A "streak" means days in a row you practice. Why do you think it is good to practice every day? Write one reason.
