My Verb Helper

Look at me anytime. Your message is more important than perfect grammar.
1. Find the helper.
I am working. I will work.
2. − Add not.
I am not working.
3. ? Move it to the front.
Are you working?
No helper? Use do / does / did.
I work. → I don't work. → Do you work?
