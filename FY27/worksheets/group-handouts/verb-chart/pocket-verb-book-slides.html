My Pocket Verb Book

7 verb tenses. One small book. You write it.

You need

  1. 8 cards (cover + 1 to 7)
  2. Scissors
  3. A pencil
  4. A stapler (we share)

Make the book · Step 1

Check your cards.

You have 8 cards. Put them in order.

Cover1 · Present simple2 · Past simple3 · Future simple4 · Present continuous5 · Past continuous6 · Future continuous7 · Present perfect

Make the book · Step 2

Cut off the stripes.

Cut on the thick black line ✂.

Keep the colored bar. It is your tab.

Do it on all 7 tense cards and the cover.

Careful! Every card is a different length. Cut on the line.

Make the book · Step 3

Stack your cards.

Cover on top, then 1, 2, 3 … 7.

Line up the tops.

Can you see all 7 tabs? If not, check the order.

Make the book · Step 4

Staple at the top.

2 staples in the gray strip: one on the left, one on the right.

Write your name on the cover.

1 Card 1 · Present simple

Explain it to a partner: Where is it? Is it a dot, many dots, or a line?

Answer: many dots. A routine. It happens again and again.

1 Card 1 · Present simple

Fill in the chart.

Choose your own verbs: one regular, one irregular. Write all 3 columns, then check the formula. Our example uses work and eat.

+ Affirmative− Negative? Question
Formulasubject + V1subject + don't + V1Do + subject + V1?
Regular example: workI work at a hotel.I don't work on Sunday.Do you work on Sunday?
Irregular example: eatI eat breakfast at 7.I don't eat meat.Do you eat meat?
he / she / it:She works. · She doesn't work. · Does she work?
