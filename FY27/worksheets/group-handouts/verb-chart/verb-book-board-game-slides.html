Tuesday · Whiteboard game

See the time. Build the sentence.

How well do you know your pocket verb book?

How to play

  1. Close your verb book.
  2. Everyone writes on a whiteboard. Talk with your group.
  3. Teacher says “1, 2, 3, boards up!”
  4. Right = 1 point for your group.

Level 2 of 5

Write it

Look at the timeline. Write the sentence. Use the person and the verb.

2 Level 2 · Write it · 1 of 3

she + cook

Card 1 · Present simple · many dots
Example: She cooks dinner every day. · She cooks on Sundays.

Check: she → V1-s: cooks

2 Level 2 · Write it · 2 of 3

they + watch TV

Card 5 · Past continuous · a line
Example: They were watching TV at 8:00 last night.

Check: they → were + V-ing

2 Level 2 · Write it · 3 of 3

he + eat sushi

Card 7 · Present perfect · a dot + an arc to NOW
Example: He has eaten sushi. · He has eaten sushi two times.

Check: he → has + V3 (eat · ate · eaten)

Level 3 of 5

Flip it

Make it negative (−) or a question (?).

3 Level 3 · Flip it · 1 of 3

They went to the park.

Make it − negative.

They didn’t go to the park.

Watch out! After didn’t, use V1: didn’t went → didn’t go

3 Level 3 · Flip it · 2 of 3

She works on Sundays.

Make it ? a question.

Does she work on Sundays?

Watch out! The -s moves to does: Does she works? → Does she work?

3 Level 3 · Flip it · 3 of 3

She has seen the ocean.

Make it ? a question.

Has she seen the ocean?

Watch out! Move has to the front. Keep V3: Has she saw? → Has she seen?

they + visit New York  →  +   −   ?
