1Present simple

Meaning: Habits, routines, and facts.

Helpful time clues: every day · usually · always · on Mondays

Draw when: I eat breakfast every day.
Stamp: many dots. A routine. It happens again and again.

+ Affirmative− Negative? Question
Formulasubject + V1subject + don't + V1Do + subject + V1?
Regular e.g. workI work at a hotel.I don't work on Sunday.Do you work on Sunday?
Irregular e.g. eatI eat breakfast at 7.I don't eat meat.Do you eat meat?

he / she / it: She works. · She doesn't work. · Does she work?

My sentence: your own idea, true about your life. Different people, different sentences: I drink coffee every morning. · My son plays soccer on Saturdays. · I don't watch TV at night.

Watch out! For this action: I am work. → I work.

2Past simple

Meaning: Finished actions in the past.

Helpful time clues: yesterday · last week · 2 days ago · in 2020

Draw when: I ate breakfast yesterday.
Stamp: one dot. An event at a point in time.

+ Affirmative− Negative? Question
Formulasubject + V2subject + didn't + V1Did + subject + V1?
Regular e.g. workI worked yesterday.I didn't work yesterday.Did you work yesterday?
Irregular e.g. eatI ate breakfast.I didn't eat breakfast.Did you eat breakfast?

My sentence: your own idea, true about your life. Different people, different sentences: I visited my sister last weekend. · I moved here in 2019. · I didn't sleep well last night.

Watch out! After did, use V1: Did you went? → Did you go?

3Future simple

Meaning: Predictions, promises, and decisions made now.

Helpful time clues: tomorrow · next week · later · soon

Draw when: I will eat breakfast tomorrow.
Stamp: one dot. An event at a point in time.

+ Affirmative− Negative? Question
Formulasubject + will + V1subject + won't + V1Will + subject + V1?
Regular e.g. workI will work tomorrow.I won't work tomorrow.Will you work tomorrow?
Irregular e.g. eatI will eat later.I won't eat later.Will you eat later?

My sentence: your own idea, true about your life. Different people, different sentences: I will call my mom tonight. · It will be cold tomorrow. · I won't be late.

Watch out! No -s after will: She will works. → She will work.
