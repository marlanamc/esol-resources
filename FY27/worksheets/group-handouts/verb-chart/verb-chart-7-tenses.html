My Verb Chart

7 tenses, one verb: work
This chart is a helper. You do not need to memorize it. Keep it with you. Look at it when you write or speak. If you make a mistake, that's OK. People understand you.

3 big ideas (they work for every tense on this page)

1. Find the helper.
I am working.
I will work.
I have worked.
2. − Add not after it.
I am not working.
I will not work.
I have not worked.
3. ? Move it to the front.
Are you working?
Will you work?
Have you worked?
No helper? Simple present and simple past have a hidden helper: do / does / did. You see it only in − and ?  I work. → I don't work. → Do you work?

Try It: One Step at a Time

Use your chart. Looking is OK!

A. Make the − and the ?

Find the helper. For −, add not. For ?, move the helper to the front. No helper? Use do / does / did.
+−?
Present simple She plays soccer. She doesn't play soccer. Does she play soccer?
Past simple They played soccer yesterday.
Future simple We will play soccer tomorrow.
Present continuous He is playing soccer now.
Past continuous You were playing soccer at 6:00 last night.
Future continuous I will be playing soccer at 6:00 tomorrow.
Present perfect She has played soccer for 10 years.
