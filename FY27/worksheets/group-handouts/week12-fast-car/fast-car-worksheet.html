Her parents (V2, past) ➜ her life now. Write the verb in V1-s.

a. How much does she earn in one week?
b. How much does she earn in one month (4 weeks)?
c. How much does she spend every month?
d. How much money is left every month?
e. She needs $2,600 for a new apartment. How many months does she need to save?

f. Give her advice. Write 2 sentences: She should / must … and If she …, she will …

You make up the advice. Three students, three different ideas:
She should cook at home. If she stops buying snacks, she will save $50.
She must work more hours. If she works 30 hours, she will earn $450 a week.
She shouldn't drive so much. If she takes the bus, she will spend less on gas.
