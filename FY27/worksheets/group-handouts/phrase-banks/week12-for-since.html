How Long? For and Since

Keep this page. Use it to write on the back.

1. for or since? Both answer How long? Both use have / has + V3.

for
+ how much time
5 minutes · 2 weeks · 10 years

2. How does it work? The important words are in black.

A. Ask: How long have you + V3?
How longhaveyoulivedhere?
How long has she worked there?
✗ How long do you live here?
B. for + how much time
Ihave livedherefor7 years.
I’ve known her for a long time.
✗ since 7 years
C. since + the start point
Ihave workedtheresince2021.
…since Monday / since I was 15.
✗ for 2021
D. Not since then: haven’t + V3
Ihaven’thada vacationsince 2023.
She hasn’t been to the beach for a year.
✗ I don’t have a vacation since 2023.
Remember: V3: live → lived · know → known · be → been · have → had  |  he / she / it → has
