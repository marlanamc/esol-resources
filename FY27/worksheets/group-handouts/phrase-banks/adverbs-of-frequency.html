2. Where does it go? The adverb is in black.

A. Most verbs: BEFORE the verb
Iusuallydrinkcoffee.
She never eats breakfast.
✗ I drink usually coffee.
B. am / is / are: AFTER the verb
Iamalwaystired.
He is sometimes late.
✗ I always am tired.
C. Negative: don't / doesn't + usually
Idon'tusuallycook.
She doesn't often watch TV.
Never is already negative: ✗ I don't never cook.
D. Questions: How often...? / ...ever...?
How oftendo youcook?
Do you ever take a nap?
→ Yes, I sometimes take a nap.
Remember: he / she / it + V1-s → My friend usually drinks tea.  |  Sometimes can go first: Sometimes I take the bus.
