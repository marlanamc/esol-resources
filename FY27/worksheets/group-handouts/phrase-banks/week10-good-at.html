2. How does it work? The important words are in black.

A. good at + V-ing
I’mgood atcookingrice.
She’s good at fixing cars.
✗ I’m good at cook.
B. good at + a noun
I’mgood atmath.
He’s bad at names.
✗ I’m good in math.
C. Question: Are you good at…?
Areyougood atdrawing?
→ Yes, I am! / I’m OK at it. / Not really.
✗ Do you good at drawing?
D. interested in + V-ing / noun
I’minterested inlearningcomputers.
She’s interested in nursing.
✗ I’m interesting in art.
Remember: -ing spelling: make → making · swim → swimming  |  not very good at is a polite way to say bad at.
