Can, Should, Must

Keep this page. Use it to write on the back.

1. What does it mean? How much choice do you have?

you chooseno choice
can able / possibleI can speak Spanish.
should a good ideaYou should call back.
must a strong ruleYou must pay rent.
can't not ableI can't read small letters.
shouldn't a bad ideaYou shouldn't click it.
must not never do itYou must not share your PIN.

2. How does it work? The modal is in black.

A. Modal + V1. No "to".
Icancallmy mom.
You should check your messages.
✗ I can to call my mom.
B. he / she / it: no -s
Shecancallher son.
He must work on Saturday.
✗ She cans call.   ✗ She can calls.
C. Negative: can't / shouldn't / must not + V1
Ican'tdrive.
You shouldn't answer unknown numbers.
✗ I don't can drive.   ✗ I no can drive.
D. Questions: the modal goes first
Canyouhelpme?
Should I call back? → Yes, you should.
✗ Do you can help me?
Remember: can = possible  ·  should = a good idea  ·  must = a rule
