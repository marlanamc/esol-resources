2. How does it work? The important words are in black.

A. Can count: How many + -s
How manyeggsdo you need?
→ a few eggs / six eggs
✗ How many egg?
B. Can’t count: How much (no -s)
How muchricedo you need?
→ a little rice / a bag of rice
✗ How much rices?
C. Money: How much is / does… cost?
How muchisit?
How much does the bus cost?
✗ How many is it?
D. Both: a lot of / some / any
I havea lot offriends / time.
Do you have any milk? → I don’t have any.
✗ I have many money.
Remember: Count it with a container: a bottle of water · a bag of rice · a loaf of bread · two cups of coffee
