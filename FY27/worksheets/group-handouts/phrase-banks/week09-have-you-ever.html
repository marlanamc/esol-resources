2. How does it work? The important words are in black.

A. Ask: Have you ever + V3?

B. Yes: I have + V3

C. No: I have never + V3
Ihaveneverseensnow.
No, I haven’t. / I’ve never flown.
✗ I haven’t never seen snow.
D. Tell more: When did you + V1?
When didyougo?

Remember: he / she / it → has: She has never driven a truck.  |  once · twice · many times go at the end.
