How to Use My ESOL Class

1

Open the app

On your phone, open Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). Type this website:

myesolclass.com

Save it on your phone. Then the app is on your home screen, like other apps.

iPhone (Safari)Tap Share (the box with an arrow ↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
Android (Chrome)Tap ⋮ (3 dots, top right) → Add to Home screen or Install app.
2

Sign in

  1. Type your Username.
  2. Type your Password.
  3. Tap Sign In.
  4. The first time, the app may ask you to make a new password. Write it in a safe place.

My practice week

Did you practice English at home? Listen, watch, read, write, or speak. Tap + Log, pick one, and write 1 sentence about it.

Example: I watched a video in English. You get points!

Have a question?

Open Map → this week → My question for class. Type in English or your language. Tap Send. I answer in class.

Don't want your name on it? Check Don't show my name.

Remember: Small practice every day is better than a lot one day. Mistakes are OK! If the app is confusing, ask me or a classmate. We will help you.
