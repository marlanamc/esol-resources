Getting Ready

Just · Already · Yetfrom Week 10
alreadyhave / has+already+V3I have already bought the food.
justhave / has+just+V3She has just made a pie.
yethaven't / hasn't+V3+… yetI haven't cleaned yet.

Read the models. Two people, two different answers.

Mandy
Family dinner at her house tomorrow

It is Wednesday night, and I am very busy! My family is coming tomorrow. I have already bought the turkey. I have already cleaned the house. I have just made two pies, and the kitchen smells so good. I haven't set the table yet. I haven't called my sister yet. She is bringing the rice.

Kevin
Works at the food pantry. No big dinner.

I don't cook on Thanksgiving. I work at the food pantry. Today we have already given out 100 turkeys! I have just gotten home, and I am tired. I have already bought my bus ticket to my mom's house. I haven't packed my bag yet.

Write about YOUR week. What have you already done? What haven't you done yet?
No Thanksgiving for you? No problem. Write about your work, your home, or your weekend.
Ideas: cleaned · cooked · bought food · finished work · called my family · done the laundry · paid my bills · studied English
I have already ___.I have just ___.I haven't ___ yet.My ___ has already ___.
Check:alreadyjustyethave / has + V35+ sentences

Today Is Thanksgiving

Right Now + Usuallyfrom Weeks 1 + 6
right nowam / is / are+V1-ingMy kids are watching TV.
every yearusually / always+V1We usually eat at 4:00.
he / shealways / never+V1-sMy mom always cries.

Read the models. Two people, two different answers.

Mandy
Family dinner at her house

Today is Thanksgiving. It is 2:00, and my house is very loud! Right now, my husband is cooking the turkey. My kids are watching the parade on TV. My sister is making rice and beans. Every year, we usually eat at 4:00. My mother always says a prayer before dinner. We never finish all the food!

Kevin
At his mom's apartment

Today I am at my mom's apartment. Right now, she is sleeping on the couch, and I am washing the dishes. We are not eating turkey. We are eating fish soup, like in our country. On holidays, we usually call our family back home. My mom always cries a little. I sometimes cry too.

Write about YOUR day today. What is happening right now? What do you usually do on a holiday?
Working today? Write about your job right now!
Ideas: cooking · eating · watching TV · working · resting · talking on the phone · playing · sleeping

Mandy
Family dinner at her house

Yesterday was a great day. My family came at 1:00. We ate turkey, rice and beans, and pie. My son helped me in the kitchen. After dinner, we played cards and watched football. Everyone went home at 9:00. I didn't wash the dishes. I was too tired! Today I am eating leftovers.

Kevin
Visited his mom

Yesterday I took the bus to my mom's house. The trip was two hours. My mom cooked fish soup, and we talked all day. I didn't watch football. I don't like football! At night, we looked at old photos. I felt happy and a little homesick.

Write about YESTERDAY (Thursday). Where did you go? What did you eat? Who did you see? How did you feel?

Holidays I Know

Have You Ever…?from Week 9
in my lifehave / has+V3I have eaten tamales.
not in my lifehave never+V3I have never cooked a turkey.
the storyV2++ whenI went in 2019.

Read the models. Two people, two different answers.

Mandy
Thanksgiving every year

I have celebrated Thanksgiving many times. I have never cooked a turkey alone. My husband always helps! I have been to the big parade in New York one time. I went in 2019. It was very cold, but I loved the giant balloons. Have you ever seen the parade?

Kevin
A different holiday from home

In my country, we don't have Thanksgiving. Our big holiday is in December. Have you ever eaten tamales? I have eaten a lot of tamales! My grandmother made them every Christmas. I have also tried turkey here, but I have never eaten pumpkin pie. Maybe next year!

Write about a holiday from YOUR country, or a holiday you love. What have you done? What have you never done?
Then add one story sentence with V2: when did it happen?
Ideas: eaten · cooked · danced · seen · been to · celebrated · made · tried · given · worn

Advice for a Friend

Can · Should · Mustfrom Week 8
good ideashould / shouldn't+V1You should bring a pie.
it's OKcan+V1You can say, “No, thank you.”
very importantmust / must not+V1You must buy boots.

Read the models. Two people, two different answers.

Mandy
Letter to Rosa: her first Thanksgiving dinner

Dear Rosa, You are going to your coworker's house for Thanksgiving. Here is my advice. You should bring something small, like flowers or a pie. You shouldn't arrive very early. You can try a little of everything. You can say, “No, thank you,” if you don't like something. And you must say thank you to the cook! Love, Mandy

Kevin
Letter to Luis: his first winter here

Dear Luis, Welcome to Massachusetts! Winter is coming, so here is my advice. You must buy a warm coat and boots. You can find cheap coats at the thrift store. You should wear a hat every day. You shouldn't walk on ice in sneakers. You must not park on the street in a snow emergency. See you soon, Kevin

Write a letter to a friend. Choose:  A. their first Thanksgiving dinner   B. their first winter here
You make up the friend's name! No “to” after can / should / must: You should to bring → You should bring.
Ideas: bring · wear · buy · try · say · arrive · drive · walk · call · ask
Dear ___,You should ___.You shouldn't ___.You can ___.You must ___.
Check:should / shouldn'tcanmustV1 after (no to, no -s)5+ sentences

Looking Ahead to December

If… Will…from Week 7
the if partIf+V1 / V1-s+,If it snows,
the resultwill+V1my kids will build a snowman.
an ideaLet's+V1Let's have a party!

Read the models. Two people, two different answers.

Mandy
Plans with her kids

December is coming! If it snows this month, my kids will build a snowman. If I have a day off, I will take them to see the lights downtown. If the weather is nice, we will walk to the park. If I see my classmates on Tuesday, I will tell them about my pies!

Kevin
Saving money for a trip

In December, I will work a lot at the food pantry. If I save money, I will buy a plane ticket. If I buy a ticket, I will visit my family in January. If it is very cold, I will stay home and study English. Let's have a party in December, class!

Write about YOUR December. You make up the “if” part. It can be big or small!
Ideas: snows · have time · get a day off · save money · feel tired · see my family · get paid
