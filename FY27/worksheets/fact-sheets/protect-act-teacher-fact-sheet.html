How Staff Can Help

Staff Fact Sheet · Oct. 21, 2026 · Draft
Every conversation
  • Language: offer their preferred language (MIRA: English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, Portuguese).
  • Consent: ask what help they want and how to contact them safely. Ask before sharing info or referring.
  • Privacy: follow program procedures. No case details in class notes or group talk.
  • Time-sensitive: for detention, transfer, or a court date coming up, a legal referral is the best next step.
  • Legal questions: leave warrant interpretation and court filings to qualified legal providers. Do not guarantee outcomes.
Student handout (The PROTECT Act: What to Know): it’s a good idea to have counsel review it and check for updated guidance before sharing.
