The PROTECT Act: What to Know

Fact Sheet · October 2026

A new Massachusetts law about immigration. Some parts of the law have already started. More parts start on November 3. This sheet is general information, not legal advice.

Aug. 5Law signed. Job and child care rules start.
Sept. 4Police and court rules start.
Nov. 3Courthouse, jail, and U / T visa rules start.
1Can local police ask about my status?
  • Since Sept. 4Usually no. Most MA police and court workers can’t ask or write it down.
  • There are some exceptions.
  • This rule is for police and courts, not every job or school.
You can: Something happened with police? A lawyer can help you understand it.
2Can ICE arrest someone at court?
  • From Nov. 3At a MA courthouse, ICE needs a judge’s warrant or order.
  • No immigration arrests inside a courtroom.
  • This does not mean court is always safe. An ICE warrant is different from a judge’s warrant.
You can: Worried about a court date? It is a good idea to talk to your lawyer before it.
3My family member was detained or moved.
  • It helps to call their lawyer or legal help as soon as you can.
  • From Nov. 3Some MA jails must update records within 6 hours, and try to tell the lawyer and family within 6 hours of a move.
  • Not every holding place must do this.
4Will I know if ICE checks records at my job?
  • Since Aug. 5Usually yes. Your employer must tell every worker in writing within 48 hours after ICE tells the employer about an inspection.
  • This is for I-9 forms and other work records.
You can: Get help from a workers’ group or lawyer. It is best to talk to a lawyer before you change or give any documents.

The PROTECT Act: Work and Family

Fact Sheet · October 2026
5My boss or landlord threatened me.
  • A lawyer can tell you if a U visa or T visa could help you. These visas are for some victims of crimes or trafficking.
  • Unpaid wages or bad housing don’t automatically count.
  • From Nov. 3MA agencies must answer faster when you ask for a visa support form. (The form is not the visa.)
You can: Ask your teacher for a private talk. Your teacher can help you find a lawyer.
6Who can care for my child in an emergency?
  • Caregiver form (caregiver authorization affidavit): a trusted adult makes school and health decisions. No court. You are still the parent.
  • Standby guardian: a different plan. It goes through court.
  • Many families don’t need both.
You can: Read the family plan guide below. A lawyer can help you choose.
7An officer is not following this law.
  • The MA Attorney General’s office takes complaints.
You can: Write down the date, time, and place. Keep any papers. Talk to your teacher if you want help reporting it.
