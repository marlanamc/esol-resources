Leading Station 2: SPEAK (Which is better?)

Packet pages 4–5

Goal: use -er / more … than to give reasons, and the -est / the most for number 1. Pronunciation: than is weak (“thn”). Common errors: more cheaper, more better, the most big, bigger that.

3 minQuiet than. Model: The BUS is CHEAPer thn a TAXi. Everyone repeats.
4 minA. Listen. Read the sentences below. They circle compare 2 or number 1.
9 minB. Debate. 2–3 topics. Each person: a side + 2 reasons. Keep it friendly and fast.
5 minC. Who is number 1? At the table. Then 2 sentences each.
4 minD + E. Talk, then each person reads you their two sentences.

A. Read these sentences

1The bus is cheaper than a taxi.compare 2
2This is the cheapest store.number 1
3Winter is colder than fall.compare 2
4July is the hottest month.number 1
5Rent is more expensive than last year.compare 2
6Coffee had the biggest change.number 1
7My new job is better than my old job.compare 2
8That was the best day of my life!number 1

When they say it wrong

  • more cheaper → short word: -er only. Long word: more only. Never both.
  • good → better → the best, bad → worse → the worst.
  • Fix one thing, then move on.
Learner: “The city is more better.”
You: “The city is better? Why is it better?”
