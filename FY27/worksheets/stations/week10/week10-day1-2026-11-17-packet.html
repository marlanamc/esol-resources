Quick or long?

i  ship · sit · live

Short and quick. Your mouth is relaxed. Don’t smile.

ee  sheep · seat · leave

Long. Smile wide, like a photo: cheeeese!

Try it: Put your fingers on the corners of your mouth. Say sit, then seat. On ee, your mouth moves out to a smile.

STATION 3: TALK CARDSHave you ever…? · with your group
Page 6

How to play

  1. 1. Put the cards face down on the table.
  2. 2. Take a card. Ask the person on your left: “Have you ever …?”
  3. 3. They answer: Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t. / No, never.
  4. 4. Yes? Ask one more question with did: When did you…? Where did you…? Did you like it?
  5. 5. No? Ask: “Do you want to?” Then the next person takes a card.
Have you ever + V3?
any time in your life
When / Where did you + V1?
one finished time
A: Have you ever eaten sushi?   B: Yes, I have.
A: When did you eat it?     B: I ate it last year at my friend’s house.
A: Did you like it?       B: Yes, I did!

Write it down
