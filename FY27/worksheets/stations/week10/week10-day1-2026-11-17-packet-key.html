Quick or long?

i  ship · sit · live

Short and quick. Your mouth is relaxed. Don’t smile.

ee  sheep · seat · leave

Long. Smile wide, like a photo: cheeeese!

Try it: Put your fingers on the corners of your mouth. Say sit, then seat. On ee, your mouth moves out to a smile.

How to play

  1. 1. Pick a question. Ask the person on your left. Everyone puts an ✗ on it.
  2. 2. They answer: Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t. / No, never.
  3. 3. Yes? Ask one more question with did: When did you…? Where did you…? Did you like it?
  4. 4. No? Ask: “Do you want to?” Then that person picks the next question. Stuck? Look at page 5.
