Leading Station 2: SPEAK (i / ee)

Packet pages 4–5

Spanish, Portuguese, Haitian Creole, and Arabic have one i sound, close to English ee, so live and leave sound the same. Goal: hear it, then make it.

3 minQuick or long? Fingers on the corners of the mouth: sit / seat. Exaggerate the smile on ee.
5 minA. Listen and circle. Say each word twice, naturally. Then check.
4 minB. Partner game. Walk around. Model once, slowly, if needed.
3 minC. Which sentence? Read the sentences below.
5 minD. Read aloud. One sentence each. Fix only i / ee.
5 minE. Talk. Push for a full sentence and one follow-up.

A. Say these words

1sheep5heat
2sit6chip
3leave7bean
4fill8still

C. Say these sentences

  1. b. I want to live here.
  2. a. Is that a ship?
  3. a. These are cheap.
  4. b. Please feel the cup.

When they say it wrong

  • Point to your smile for ee, relax your face for i.
  • Fix one word, then move on. Keep it light.
Learner: “I want to leev in Boston.”
You: “Oh, you want to live in Boston? Where?”

Adjust the level

  • Beginner: repeat after you, word by word.
  • Stronger: they say a word for the group to circle.
