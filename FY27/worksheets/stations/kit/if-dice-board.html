If… Dice

Roll 2 dice. Die 1 = row ↓  Die 2 = column →. Finish the sentence out loud. The group asks one more question: “Why?” “And then?” Row 6: ask the person on your left.

If… Dice: Help card

Stuck? Find your square and read the example. Then change it to make it true for you.
ZEROIf…If the T is late, I text my boss.If my kids are sick, I stay home with them.If you park on the street in a snow emergency, your car gets towed.If it snows a lot, I shovel the sidewalk.If you don’t pay a bill on time, you pay a late fee.If I don’t understand someone, I ask them to repeat.
ZERO…ifI call my family back home if I have free time on Sunday.I order takeout if I’m too tired to cook.I take an Uber if I miss the last bus.I feel stressed if the rent is late.I don’t sleep well if I drink coffee at night.My kids are happy if we go to the park.
FIRSTIf…If it snows tomorrow, I will wear my boots.If my landlord doesn’t fix the heat this week, I will call 311.If I get a day off next week, I will sleep late!If my family visits next summer, I will show them Boston.If I finish my work early tonight, I will watch a movie.If I get a raise this year, I will save for a car.
FIRST…ifI will cook something special this weekend if my sister visits.I won’t go to work tomorrow if my son is still sick.I will buy a new phone if my old phone breaks.I will take the kids to the beach if it’s hot on Saturday.I will call the doctor if I don’t feel better tomorrow.I will go to bed early tonight if I’m tired after class.
FIRSTnotIf my boss doesn’t let me leave early tomorrow, I won’t come to class.If it doesn’t rain this weekend, I will go to the park.I won’t buy a car this year if gas is too expensive.If my package doesn’t come today, I will call the store.I won’t be hungry tonight if I eat a big lunch.If the bus doesn’t come in ten minutes, I will walk.
ASKa partnerIf it rains on Saturday, I will stay home and watch TV.If I get a day off, I will go to the beach.If friends come over, I will cook rice and beans.If I get good news tonight, I will call my mom.If I get paid tomorrow, I will buy new shoes.If I can’t sleep, I read or listen to music.
