Volunteer rules

  • You must sign up before your shift.
  • You must sign in at the front desk when you arrive.
  • You must wear closed-toe shoes. No sandals.
  • You should bring a water bottle.
  • You should wear old clothes. Things can get dirty!
  • You can’t bring children under 12 to a shift.
  • Can’t come? Please text us 24 hours before your shift.

How to sign up

  • Text VOLUNTEER to 55190
  • or sign up online: eastsidepantry.example.org

Getting here

  • Bus 7 stops in front.
  • Free parking in the church lot on Saturday only.

We speak: English · Español · Português · Kreyòl ayisyen
