Every week we have: rice · beans · pasta · canned food · bread · eggs · milk

Pantry rules

  • You must sign in at the front desk.
  • You can come one time a week.
  • First visit? You should bring proof of address: a bill or a letter with your name.
  • No ID? That’s OK. You can still get food.
  • You should bring your own bags.
  • You can bring your children.

Good to know

  • Free food from a food pantry does not hurt your immigration case.
  • Bus 7 stops in front.
  • Heavy bags? Ask us. We can help.

Want to help out?

  • Volunteers are welcome. Text VOLUNTEER to 55190.

We speak: English · Español · Português · Kreyòl ayisyen
