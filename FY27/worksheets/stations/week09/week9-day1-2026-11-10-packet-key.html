Can or can’t? Listen to the beat.

can

da da DUM

can is short and quiet. The verb is loud.

can’t

I CAN’T swim.

da DUM da

can’t is long and loud. You often don’t hear the t!

How to play

  1. 1. Pick a problem. Read it out loud: “I have a problem. … What should I do?” Everyone puts an ✗ on it.
  2. 2. Everyone gives advice: You should … · You shouldn’t … · You can … + V1. Stuck? Look at the phrase bank on page 5.
  3. 3. The reader picks the best advice: “Good idea! Thank you.” That person gets a point ( | ).
  4. 4. The next person picks a problem. Most points when the timer rings wins!
