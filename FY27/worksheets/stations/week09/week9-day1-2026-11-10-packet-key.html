Can or can’t? Listen to the beat.

can

I can SWIM.

da da DUM

can is short and quiet. The verb is loud.

can’t

I CAN’T swim.

da DUM da

can’t is long and loud. You often don’t hear the t!

Try it: Clap the beat. I can SWIM (clap on SWIM). I CAN’T swim (clap on CAN’T).

How to play

  1. 1. Put the cards in a pile, problem side down.
  2. 2. Take a card. Read the problem out loud: “I have a problem. …”
  3. 3. Everyone gives advice. Use should, shouldn’t, or can + V1. Stuck? Look at the back of the card for ideas.
  4. 4. You pick the best advice. That person keeps the card.
  5. 5. Next person takes a card. Most cards when the timer rings wins!

Give advice

You should …You shouldn’t … You can …Maybe you can …

Answer the advice

Good idea! Thank you.Hmm. I can’t. I … That’s the best advice!Can you say that again?
Example:
Ana (reads a card): I have a problem. Food is very expensive this month.
Juan: You should go to a food pantry.   Mei: You can buy rice and beans. They’re cheap.
Ana: Good idea, Juan! Thank you. (Juan keeps the card.)

Remember: should + V1. No to.   ✗ You should to go.   ✓ You should go.
