My money this week

Write about yourself or an imaginary person. Use have/has + V3, and cost or spend.

Example: I have already paid my phone bill this month.

1. Already: a bill or payment you finished before today.

Example: I have already paid the rent.

2. Not yet: something you want to buy, but haven’t bought.

Example: I haven’t bought a new phone yet.

3. Just: something you did a moment ago.

Example: I have just finished page 9.

4. Cost: something that costs a lot here.

Example: Rent costs a lot in Boston.

5. Ask a partner: Have you … yet? Write their answer.

Example: Have you made a budget yet? No, not yet.
Check together: underline have or has. Circle the V3. Is yet at the end?
