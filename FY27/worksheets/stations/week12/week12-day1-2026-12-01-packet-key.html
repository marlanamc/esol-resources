STATION 3: READTwo Charges for One Phone · with your group
Page 6
Words to know
receipt = the paper that shows what you paid
debit card = a card that takes money from your bank
bank app = your bank on your phone
double charge = you pay two times for one thing
business days = Monday to Friday, not holidays
due = you must pay by this day

Rafael works at a bakery. He is good at saving money. Every month, he deposits part of his paycheck into his savings account. In November, he finally had enough money for a new phone.

On Saturday, he went to CityTech, an electronics store. The phone cost $349.99. He paid with his debit card. “You should keep your receipt,” the cashier said. “If there is a problem, you must have it to return the phone.”

On Monday, Rafael checked his bank app. He saw two charges from CityTech: $349.99 and $349.99. “That’s $699.98!” he said. “I only bought one phone.” Now he had only $85 in his account, and his rent was due on Friday.

He called his friend Lucía. “Have you ever had a double charge?” he asked. “Yes, once,” she said. “You should call the store first. If they don’t help, you can dispute the charge with your bank.”

Rafael went back to CityTech with his receipt. The manager looked at the computer. “You’re right. The machine charged you two times. I’m sorry. We will refund $349.99. It can take 5 to 7 business days.”

“Five to seven days? My rent is due on Friday,” Rafael said. The manager gave him a paper with a refund number. “If the money isn’t in your account by next Tuesday, call your bank and give them this number.”

While he was there, Rafael looked at his new phone case. It was the wrong size. “Can I exchange this case?” he asked. “Sure,” the manager said. “You can exchange it for 14 days with the receipt.” He got the right case.

On Thursday, the $349.99 was back in his account. Rafael paid his rent on time. Now he checks his bank app every week. “If you see a strange charge, don’t wait,” he tells his coworkers. “Call the store or your bank right away.”

First: Read the story alone. Read it 2 times. Then answer. The ¶ number tells you which paragraph to look in.

Part A: Think about it

The answers are not always in one sentence. Think, then write. ★ = challenge.

  1. 1. How does Rafael save money? (¶1)
    He deposits part of his paycheck into savings every month.
  2. 2. Why should you keep your receipt? (¶2)
    If there is a problem, you must have it to return the item.
  3. 3. What was the problem? How much did CityTech take in all? (¶3)
    They charged him two times: $699.98.
  4. 4. What did Lucía say to do first? Then what? (¶4)
    Call the store first. If they don’t help, dispute it with the bank.
  5. 5. ★ Why was Rafael worried about “5 to 7 business days”? (¶3, 6)
    His rent was due Friday. He only had $85. The money might come late.
  6. 6. ★ What does Rafael do differently now? (¶8)
    He checks his bank app every week.
