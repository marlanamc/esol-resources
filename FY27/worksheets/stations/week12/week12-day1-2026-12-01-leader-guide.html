Leading Station 2: SPEAK (B/V + i/ee)

Packet pages 4–5

A review of Weeks 8 and 10. Mixing both sounds in one sentence is the hard part (very cheap, best vest). Use the same cues: lips / teeth for B/V, relaxed / smile for i/ee.

3 minWarm up. very cheap / berry chip. Exaggerate both.
5 minA. Listen and circle. Say each word twice, naturally. Then check.
4 minB. Partner game. Walk around.
3 minC. Which sentence? Read the sentences below.
5 minD. Read aloud. One sentence each. Fix one sound per person.
5 minE. Talk. Full sentence + one follow-up.

A. Say these words

1vote5best
2live6feel
3very7van
4seat8cheap

C. Say these sentences

  1. a. I need to leave the bank.
  2. a. Is this your vest?
  3. b. The cheap ones are on sale.
  4. b. They counted the votes.

When they say it wrong

  • Point: lips or teeth? Smile or relax?
  • Fix one word, then move on.
  • In Part E, repeat it back correctly. Don’t stop them.
Learner: “I bought a bery chip coat.”
You: “A very cheep coat? Nice! Where?”
