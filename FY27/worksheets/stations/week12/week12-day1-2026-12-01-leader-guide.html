Leading Station 2: SPEAK (short i / long ee)

Packet pages 3–4

Short i (ship, live, fill) is quick and relaxed. Long ee (sheep, leave, feel) is longer, with spread lips. Many learners use one sound for both, so cheap and chip or leave and live get confused at work and in stores. This matches the app’s Short i vs Long e Minimal Pair Lab this week.

3 minYour mouth knows. Fingers on the corners of the mouth: relax for ship, smile for sheep.
5 minA. Listen and circle. Say each word once, naturally. Then check.
4 minB. Partner game. Walk around. If wrong, model once: relax or smile.
3 minC. Sentences. Read each sentence with the word below.
5 minD. Read aloud. One sentence each. Fix only the i / ee words.
5 minE + F. Talk, then each person says their 3 hard words.

A. Say these words

1sheep5cheap
2live6bin
3fill7reach
4seat8it

C. Read these sentences

  1. I want to leave here.
  2. There is a ship in the picture.
  3. Can you feel it?
  4. Did you heat it?

When they say it wrong

  • Touch the corners of your mouth: smile = ee, relax = i. Let them try again.
  • Make it matter: “Cheap? Or chip? I want cheap shoes, not chip shoes!”
  • Fix one word, then move on.
Learner: “These shoes are chip.”
You: “Cheep? Smile! Yes, cheap!”

Adjust the level

  • Beginner: only Part A pairs 1–4.
  • Stronger: they read Part C for the group to circle.
