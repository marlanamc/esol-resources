Return Policy

  • Most items: return in 30 days with your receipt for a full refund.
  • No receipt? You get store credit (a gift card). You must show a photo ID.
  • Clothing: exchange for a new size or color for 60 days with your receipt.
  • Electronics: return in 14 days in the box. Opened electronics: 15% restocking fee.
  • Final sale (FS): no returns. No exchanges.
  • How you get your money: card payments go back to your card in 5–7 business days. Cash payments get cash.

Useful words at the returns desk

receipt = the paper that shows what you paid
