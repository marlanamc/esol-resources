Leading Station 2: SPEAK (13 / 30)

Packet pages 4–5

-teen numbers have stress at the end with a clear t: thirTEEN. -ty numbers have stress at the start and a soft t (like d): THIRdy. Mistakes with money and times cause real problems at stores and appointments.

3 minThe loud part. Hand up on the stressed syllable: thirTEEN / THIRty.
5 minA. Listen and circle. Say each number once, naturally. Then check.
4 minB. Partner game. Walk around. If wrong, model once with your hand.
3 minC. Prices. Read the prices below at normal speed.
5 minD. Read aloud. One sentence each. Fix only the numbers.
5 minE + F. Talk, then each person says their 3 hard numbers.

A. Say these numbers

130570
214618
350790
4168$1.50

C. Read these prices

  1. The pie costs $14.99.
  2. We spent $60.
  3. The bus is $1.70.
  4. The turkey costs $18.30.

When they say it wrong

  • Lift your hand on the stressed part. Let them try again.
  • In real life, check it: “One-three or three-zero?” Teach this. It’s a great safety phrase.
  • Fix one number, then move on.
Learner: “It costs THIRteen dollars.”
You: “ThirTEEN? One-three? OK!”

Adjust the level

  • Beginner: only 13–19 vs 30–90.
  • Stronger: they read prices for the group to write.
