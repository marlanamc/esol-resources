STATION 3: GAMEWhat are you good at? · with your group
Page 6

How to play

  1. 1. Put a small thing (a coin, an eraser) on START. That is you.
  2. 2. Roll the die. No die? Flip a coin: heads = move 1, tails = move 2.
  3. 3. Read your square. Ask the person on your left, or answer it yourself.
  4. 4. Answer with a full sentence. Then the group asks one more question.
  5. 5. First person to FINISH wins. Keep playing until the timer rings!
good at + V-ing
I’m good at cooking.
not good at + V-ing
I’m not good at singing.

Ways to answer

Yes, I am! I’m good at cooking rice. I’m OK at it. I’m getting better. Not really. I’m not good at it. No, I’m terrible at it! 😂

One more question

What do you cook?Who taught you? How often do you practice?Do you want to be good at it?

A: Are you good at dancing?   B: Yes, I am! I’m good at dancing salsa.
C: Who taught you?       B: My mother taught me.
