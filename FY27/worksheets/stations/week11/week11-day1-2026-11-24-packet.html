How to play

  1. 1. Pick a question. Ask the person on your left. Everyone puts an ✗ on it.
  2. 2. They answer with a full sentence. Stuck? Look at the phrase bank on page 5.
  3. 3. The group asks one more question: Who taught you? How often do you practice? Do you want to be good at it?
  4. 4. Then that person picks the next question. Keep playing until the timer rings!
