Station 2: SPEAK (B and V) · Guide

Week 8 · Tue 11/3/26 · packet pages 4–5

Goal: learners hear the difference between B and V, then make it. No grammar teaching needed.

B

Lips together, then pop.
No buzz. boat

V

Top teeth on bottom lip.
Buzz! vote

3 minpage 4
Lips or teeth?

SAY“B: lips together. Boat. V: teeth on your lip. Buzz. Vote.”

  • Show it big with your mouth. They watch their mouth in the phone camera.
5 minPart A
A. Listen and circle

SAY“I say one word. Circle the word you hear.”

1vote5bet
2best6vase
3van7curb
4very8vent
  • Say each word twice, at normal speed.
  • Then check answers: “Lips or teeth?”
4 minPart B
B. Group game

SAY“You say one word. Everyone points to it.”

  • You go first. Then go around the table.
3 minPart C
C. Which sentence?

SAY“I say a sentence. Circle a or b.” Read each one twice:

1. They counted the votes. (b)
3. Take my dog to the vet. (a)
2. This is my best one. (a)
4. Park near the curve. (b)
5 minpage 5
D. Read aloud
  • Go around the table. One sentence each.
  • Fix only B and V. Let other mistakes go.
5 minPart E
E. Talk together
  • Read a question. Everyone answers. Ask “Why?” to keep them talking.
  • Don’t stop them to correct here. Repeat it back the right way.

When they say it wrong

  • Point to your lips (B) or teeth (V).
  • Fix one word. Move on. Keep it light.
“I buy bejetables.” → “Oh, you buy vegetables? Which ones?”

If…

  • Too hard: they repeat after you, word by word.
  • Finish early: play B vs V Minimal Pair Lab in the app on the tablet.
  • Someone is shy: read the sentence together.
