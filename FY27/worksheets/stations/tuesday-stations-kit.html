Station 1
SPEAK
with Matt or Marlie
Goal: Use Thursday’s grammar when you talk.
  1. Play the dice game. Take turns.
  2. Read your question.
  3. Answer with a full sentence.
  4. Your group asks you one more question: “Why?” “And then?”
You can say “Pass.” You never have to answer a question.
Station 2
READ
with your group
Goal: Read, understand, and talk. No rush.
  1. Read the story alone. Read it 2 times.
  2. Answer Part A. Check with your group.
  3. Do Part B: find the grammar.
  4. Talk about Part C together.
Finished early? Find 3 new words. Ask your group what they mean.
Station 3
WRITE
with Matt or Marlie
Goal: Write about your life. Then make it better.
  1. Take this week’s writing paper.
  2. Write for 15 minutes. Use the phrase bank.
  3. Read one sentence to your group.
  4. Fix one sentence with the teacher.
Keep your paper. You can finish it at home.
Station 4
REVIEW
on your own
Goal: Practice this week’s words and verb.
  1. Take the Review paper.
  2. Do it alone or with a partner.
  3. Check your answers with a partner.
  4. Not finished? It’s homework. Bring it on Thursday.
Finished early? Open My ESOL App. Practice this week’s vocabulary.

Leading SPEAK

Matt & Marlie swap every block

Your job: get every learner talking. Talking matters more than perfect English.

First 2 minModel one turn of the dice game. Answer, then let them ask you “Why?”
~22 minTake turns around the table. Everyone answers. Everyone asks a follow-up.
Last 3 minEach learner says their best sentence again.

Please do

  • Wait 5 seconds. Silence means thinking.
  • Stuck? Start the sentence for them: “If it snows, I …”
  • Repeat it back correctly. Don't stop them to correct.
Learner: “If it will snow, I stay home.”
You: “Oh, if it snows, you will stay home? Me too!”

Adjust the level

  • Beginner: Give choices. “Will you stay home or go to work?”
  • Stronger: They make up their own if question for the group.

Leading WRITE

Matt & Marlie swap every block

Your job: help them finish a thought, then make one sentence better.

First 2 minShow one example sentence from the paper. Point to the phrase bank.
15 minQuiet writing. Walk around. Help with ideas, not spelling.
~9 minEach learner reads one sentence aloud. The group helps fix one sentence.
Last 1 min“Keep your paper. Finish at home if you want.”

When you fix

  • Fix one thing per learner. Choose this week’s grammar first.
  • Underline it. Let them try first. Then help.
  • Say something good before the fix.

Block 1 only (6:00–6:28)

  • WRITE starts almost empty. That's on purpose.
  • Greet late arrivals and send them to a group: 5–8 → READ, 9–12 → WRITE, 13+ → REVIEW.
