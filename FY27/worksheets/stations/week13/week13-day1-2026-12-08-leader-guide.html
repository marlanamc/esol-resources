Leading Station 2: SPEAK (How long…?)

Packet pages 4–5

Two goals: (1) answer How long have you…? with for / since, and (2) sound natural: for is weak (“fer”), the time is stressed. Learners often stress FOR, or say since 3 years.

3 minThe beat. Tap on stressed words: I’ve lived here fer SIX YEARS.
5 minA. For or since? Read the sentences below at normal speed (weak for!).
3 minB. Around the table. Fast chain. Fix since + length right away.
6 minC. Interview. At the table only. Push for full answers.
4 minD. Read aloud. Pairs read the interview, then switch.
4 minE + F. Talk, then each person reads you their two sentences.

A. Read these sentences

1I’ve lived here for 3 years.
2I’ve lived here since Monday.
3I’ve lived here for a long time.
4I’ve lived here since 2019.
5I’ve lived here since I was a kid.
6I’ve lived here for two weeks.
7I’ve lived here since last summer.
8I’ve lived here for six months.

When they say it wrong

  • since 3 years → hold up 3 fingers: for 3 years. Point back to a date: since 2023.
  • I live here for 6 years → recast: You’ve lived here for 6 years.
  • Fix one thing, then move on.
Learner: “I live here since 2020.”
You: “Oh, you’ve lived here since 2020? Six years!”

Station 3: WRITE (pages 6–7)

Answers vary. Look for: In + year + V2 (I moved here in 2019), have / has + V3 + for + length, have / has + V3 + since + start. Common errors: since 3 years; I have went (V2 for V3); I live here for 6 years (present for present perfect). Praise content first. Mark only for / since and V3.
