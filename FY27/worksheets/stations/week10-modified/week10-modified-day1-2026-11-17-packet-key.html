1. Have has two jobs

You know do has two jobs: I do my homework. (action) · Do you like coffee? (helper). Have is the same!

Specified Time
→ Use Past Simple: V2
You say when. It is finished.
I ate sushi yesterday.
Unspecified Time
→ Use Present Perfect: have / has + V3
You don’t say when. Any time up to now.
I have eaten sushi two times.

Ask: Can I put my finger on one day on the line?   Yes → V2.   No → have / has + V3.

D. The switch: ever → when

First you ask about any time. Then you ask about one time. The verb changes!

A:Have you ever lost your keys? (unspecified → have + V3) B:Yes, I have. A:When did you lose them? (asking for a time → did + V1) B:I lost them last month, at work! (specified → V2)
