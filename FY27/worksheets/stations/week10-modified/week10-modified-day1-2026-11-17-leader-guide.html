Leading Station 3: TIME WORDS

Packet pages 5–7 + app
7 minPage 5: why have? Start with do (they know its two jobs). Then the two have boxes and the test: the word after have. Sort together. The backpack: I have eaten sushi = “I carry this experience with me now,” so have really does mean something. For Spanish speakers: tengo un carro = own it, he comido = helper. English uses one word for both.
3 minThe two boxes (page 6). Link to the backpack: no date = backpack = have + V3. A date = the photo = V2.
4 minA. Sort. Do yesterday and ever together, then let them try. Check out loud.
5 minB. Circle. Rule: find the time word first, then pick the verb. Read each answer aloud as a group.
4 minC. Right or wrong? The big error is have + V3 + yesterday. Ask: “Is there a time word? Which box?”
4 minD. The switch. Read the model with one learner. Pairs talk first, then write. Listen for When did you…? (not When have you…?).
3 minE. App game, Round 1. They write their score on the page. Out of time? They finish it at home.
Learner: “I have went to New York last year.”
You: “Last year? That’s a finished time. So: I went to New York last year. Nice!”

The app game: Time Indicators (check it before class)

Title in the app: Time Indicators: Past Simple vs Present Perfect (activity id time-indicators-sorting, link: /activity/time-indicators-sorting). Open it on a phone before class to check it shows up for students. The page uses the app’s labels: Specified Time → Use Past Simple and Unspecified Time → Use Present Perfect.

Round 1 only. It has ever, never, yesterday, last week, ago, plus just, already, yet: a sneak peek of Thursday’s lesson. Say: “These go in the Present Perfect box. We learn them on Thursday!” Round 2 has for, since, lately (not taught yet), and Round 3 has the tricky this week / this morning. Fast finishers can try Round 2.

Page 1: Writing warm-up

Answers vary. 6 questions: How long have you…? (for / since) and Have you ever…? Each has a model. Common errors: I live in Boston for 4 years → I have lived…; since 4 years → for 4 years. A time word in the extra sentence (last month) → V2.
