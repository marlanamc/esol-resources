Warm-Up: Week 3, Tuesday

9/29/26

Parts of Speech Review — every word has a job. Can you find it?

1 Odd one out

Three words have the same job. Circle the one that is different. Write its part of speech.

Example: run · eat · happy · sleep → circle happy, write adjective

  1. 1. teacher · city · speak · family →
  2. 2. she · the · they · we →
  3. 3. achieve · belong · motivate · strong →
  4. 4. quiet · immigrant · tired · friendly →
  5. 5. a · an · the · he →
  6. 6. overcome · proud · immigrate · relocate →
2 What’s the job?

Look at the underlined word. Write N (noun), V (verb), ADJ (adjective), PRON (pronoun), or ART (article).

  1. 1. My family decided to relocate to Boston.
  2. 2. She works hard every day.
  3. 3. Learning English is difficult at first.
  4. 4. My children motivate me to study.
  5. 5. I have an English class on Tuesday.
  6. 6. My new neighbors help me feel that I belong.
Bonus round

Fast finisher? Write one sentence about you with a noun, a verb, and an adjective. Label them N, V, ADJ.

Finished? Open Class Companion and play Action or Description?
