Week 13 Quiz opens today at 6 p.m. Put the shopping trip in order.

1 Put the story in order

Write the order number (1-6) in the blank before each sentence.

  1. C. I compared the unit price of the big bag and the small bag.
  2. A. We ran out of rice, so I made a shopping list.
  3. E. I paid at the register and got my receipt.
  4. B. I drove to the grocery store.
  5. F. At home, I put the food away and threw out milk that went bad.
  6. D. The big bag was cheaper, so I stocked up on rice.
2 Circle the correct word

Circle "much" or "many" to complete each sentence correctly.

Example: How (much / many) onions do we need?

  1. 1. How (much / many) rice is left in the bag?
  2. 2. How (much / many) potatoes should I buy?
  3. 3. How (much / many) money did you spend today?
  4. 4. How (much / many) eggs are in the fridge?
  5. 5. How (much / many) milk do you drink each week?
