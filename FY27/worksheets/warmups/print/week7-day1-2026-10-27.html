Warm-Up: Week 7, Tuesday

10/27/26

Our Voice, Our Vote — civic verbs and telling fact from opinion.

1 Political ads: fact or opinion?

Write F (fact, can be checked) or O (opinion, a feeling or belief) for each statement.

Example: The election is on November 3rd. → F

  1. 1. She is the best candidate for our city.
  2. 2. You must register to vote before the deadline.
  3. 3. This candidate truly cares about families like yours.
  4. 4. Polling places open at 7:00 AM.
  5. 5. Everyone agrees this is the right choice.
  6. 6. Voters elect a new mayor every four years.
2 Fill in the blank

Use every word once. Change the verb form if needed.

represent · campaign · vote · petition · advocate · elect
  1. 1. Citizens in local elections to choose their leaders.
  2. 2. In November, voters will a new mayor.
  3. 3. She for better schools in her neighborhood.
  4. 4. We started a to fix the broken streetlights.
  5. 5. Your city council member your neighborhood in government.
  6. 6. Volunteers for safer streets in our city.
