Week 7 Quiz opens today at 6 p.m. If/then sentences about voting.

1 Find the errors

Find 8 errors in these if/then sentences. Circle the wrong word and write the correction above it.

Example: If you votes, you helps choose your leaders. → circle "votes," write "vote" above it.

Luis: If you registers before the deadline, you can vote in November.

Ana: If I don't knows the candidates, I always reads the voter guide first.

Luis: If people votes, their voice counts in city decisions.

Ana: If I has a question, I calls my city councilor's office.

Luis: If more neighbors participates, our street finally get fixed.

2 Write the verb

Write the right form. Remember: no will after if.

Example: If it snows (snow) tomorrow, I will stay (stay) home.

  1. 1. If it (rain) tomorrow, I won’t walk to the polls.
  2. 2. If my boss (not / let) me leave early, I will vote in the morning.
  3. 3. What will you do if the line (be) very long?
  4. 4. If the city (not / fix) the light, I (call) again.
  5. 5. you (vote) if you have time?
