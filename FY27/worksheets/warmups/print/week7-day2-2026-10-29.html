Warm-Up: Week 7, Thursday

10/29/26

Weekly Quiz 7 opens today at 6 p.m. If/then sentences about voting.

1 Find the errors

Find 5 errors in these if/then sentences. Circle the wrong word and write the correction above it.

Example: If you votes, you helps choose your leaders. → circle "votes," write "vote" above it.

Luis: If you registers before the deadline, you can vote in November.

Ana: If I don't knows the candidates, I always reads the voter guide first.

Luis: If people votes, their voice counts in city decisions.

Ana: If I has a question, I calls my city councilor's office.

Luis: If more neighbors participates, our street finally get fixed.

2 Contact an official

Write one short message to a city official about a problem in your neighborhood.

Example: If the streetlight stays broken, the corner will be unsafe at night. Please fix it soon.
