Understanding the News for Econ — matching words to meanings.

1 Match the word to the meaning

Write the letter of the correct meaning next to each word.

  1. 1. prices
  2. 2. wages
  3. 3. inflation
  4. 4. unemployed
  5. 5. economy
  6. 6. interest rate

A. not having a paid job right now   B. how much things cost   C. how well money, jobs, and businesses are doing   D. the money a worker earns   E. extra money a bank charges on money you borrow   F. when prices rise and money buys less

2 Read the headline

Read each short news sentence. Write one word: Good news or Bad news for workers.

  1. 1. More companies are hiring this month.
  2. 2. Grocery prices rose again this week.
  3. 3. The average wage went up this year.
  4. 4. The unemployment rate reached a new high.
