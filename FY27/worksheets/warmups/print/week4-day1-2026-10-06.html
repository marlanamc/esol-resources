Warm-Up: Week 4, Tuesday

10/6/26

Learning how to learn. Take your time and use your reference sheet.

1 This week's vocabulary

Fill in each blank with a word from the box. Use each word once.

focus     notice     compare     correct     remember     apply
  1. 1. Put other work away and on this one sentence.
  2. 2. Look closely at works. Do you the -s at the end?
  3. 3. Look for similarities and differences: these two sentences.
  4. 4. Your sentence has a mistake. Use the example to it.
  5. 5. Hide your notes and try to the verb forms from memory.
  6. 6. You learned a rule. Now it by using it in a new sentence.
2 Choose the verb form

Circle the form that fits. Look for clues such as yesterday and have.

Example: Yesterday, I (study / studied) for five minutes.

  1. 1. I (focus / focused) on one task every day.
  2. 2. Yesterday, I (notice / noticed) a new word.
  3. 3. We have (compare / compared) our answers.
  4. 4. Please (correct / corrected) this mistake.
  5. 5. Yesterday, she (remember / remembered) the rule.
  6. 6. I have (apply / applied) the rule in a new sentence.
