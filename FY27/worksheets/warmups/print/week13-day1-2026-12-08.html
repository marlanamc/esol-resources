Groceries — how long? for and since.

1 For or since?

Write "for" (a length of time) or "since" (a starting point) in each blank.

Example: I have lived here since 2021.

  1. 1. I have studied English two years.
  2. 2. She hasn’t worked at the clinic last spring.
  3. 3. We have known our neighbors we moved here.
  4. 4. Has he had his job six months?
  5. 5. I haven’t seen my family 2019.
2 Since → for

Today is December 8, 2026. Change since to for. Write how long.

Example: I have lived here since 2024. → for two years

  1. 1. She has worked at the store since 2016. → for
  2. 2. I haven’t called my sister since August. → for
  3. 3. We have known our landlord since 2021. → for
  4. 4. He has studied English since last December. → for
