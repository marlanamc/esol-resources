2 Find the errors

Find 6 errors with pronouns (I, me, my...) and articles (a, an, the). Circle each wrong word. Write the correct word above it.

Example: Him is my teacher. → circle Him, write He above it.

Luis: Hi Ana! When did you come to a United States?

Ana: Me family relocated here in 2022.

Luis: Why did you move?

Ana: My children wanted a better life. Them motivate me every day.

Luis: Where do you live now?

Ana: We live in a apartment near the school.

Luis: Do you like your neighbors?

Ana: Yes! They help I feel that I belong.

Luis: That’s great. What is you big goal this year?
