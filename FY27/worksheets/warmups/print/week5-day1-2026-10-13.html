Warm-Up: Week 5, Tuesday

10/13/26

Digital Habits — everyday routines and useful questions.

1 Maria’s learning routine

Complete each sentence with the present-simple form of the verb. Underline the subject.

Example: Maria reviews her notes every day. (review)

  1. 1. She to the app after class. (log in)
  2. 2. I the menu to find my lesson. (navigate)
  3. 3. We our lessons on a phone. (access)
  4. 4. Maria one activity each day. (complete)
  5. 5. She her answers after checking them. (submit)
2 Ask a real question

Write the question word. Use each word once.

Why · What · How · Where · How often · When
  1. 1. do you get to class? — By bus.
  2. 2. do you review your notes? — Three times a week.
  3. 3. is your doctor’s appointment? — Next Monday at 10:00.
  4. 4. do you need for work? — My uniform and my lunch.
  5. 5. do you go to English class? — At the community center.
  6. 6. is it hard to balance work and school? — Because I have three kids.

Finished? Choose one question from Task 2 and write your own answer in your notebook.
