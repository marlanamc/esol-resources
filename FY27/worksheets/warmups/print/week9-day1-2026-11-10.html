Helping + Volunteering — have you ever...?

1 Have you ever...? or Did you...?

Circle the right start. Have you ever + V3 = any time in your life. Did you + V1 = one finished time.

Example: (Have you ever / Did you) donated blood?

  1. 1. (Have you ever / Did you) volunteered at a food bank?
  2. 2. (Have you ever / Did you) help out at the cleanup last Saturday?
  3. 3. (Have you ever / Did you) signed up for a class online?
  4. 4. (Have you ever / Did you) donate clothes last winter?
  5. 5. (Have you ever / Did you) supported a friend in a hard time?
2 Fill in the blank
