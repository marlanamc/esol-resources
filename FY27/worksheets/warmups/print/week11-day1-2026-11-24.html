Warm-Up: Week 11, Tuesday

11/24/26

Short week before Thanksgiving — how long have you...?

1 For or since?

Write "for" (a length of time) or "since" (a starting point) in each blank.

Example: I have lived here since 2021.

  1. 1. I have studied English two years.
  2. 2. She has worked at the clinic last spring.
  3. 3. We have known our neighbors we moved here.
  4. 4. He has had his job six months.
  5. 5. I have set aside money January.
2 Would you rather?

Circle your answer. Then write one reason why.

  1. 1. Would you rather comparison shop at three stores or buy at the first store and save time?
  2. 2. Would you rather keep track of your spending in a notebook or on your phone?
  3. 3. Before Thanksgiving break, would you rather have more homework or more rest?

Short week! Week 11 Quiz opens Tuesday, Dec. 1 (no class Thursday for Thanksgiving); due Tuesday, Dec. 8 at 6 p.m. Happy Thanksgiving!

3 Verb forms of the week

Write the other forms of cost and spend. Use your reference sheet if you need help.

cost
spend
4 Use the right form

Look at the bold clue words. Write the correct form of cost or spend. Use your chart.

  1. 1. Every month, my phone bill $45. (cost)
  2. 2. Right now, I am too much on takeout. (spend)
  3. 3. Yesterday, the groceries $86. (cost)
  4. 4. Last weekend, we $30 at the farmers market. (spend)
  5. 5. We have $200 on school supplies since August. (spend) (Present Perfect Tense)
  6. 6. This old car has me $1,000 in repairs. (cost) (Present Perfect Tense)
5 Bonus: Your turn

Write a true sentence about you. Use the clue and the verb.

Last week + spend

Ideas: money on food · $20 on gas · time at home

Every month + cost

Ideas: my rent · my phone bill · a bus pass

I have + spend (Present Perfect Tense)

Ideas: two years here · a lot on rent
