Week 10 Quiz opens today at 6 p.m. Short i vs. long e, and have you ever...?

1 Short i or long e?

Say both words. Short i (sit) is quick. Long e (seat) is long, with a smile. Circle the word that makes sense.

Example: Nice to (meet / mitt) you!

  1. 1. Do you (live / leave) near the school?
  2. 2. I don’t want to (live / leave) early.
  3. 3. These shoes don’t (fit / feet) me.
  4. 4. Please (fill / feel) out this form.
  5. 5. Is this the (ship / sheep) to the island?
  6. 6. I can’t (sleep / slip) when it’s hot.
2 Have you ever...?

Answer in a full sentence: Yes, I have. I ... or No, I haven’t. Then ask a partner.

  1. 1. Have you ever volunteered at a school or church?
  2. 2. Have you ever donated clothing or food?
  3. 3. Have you ever signed up for a class or a team?
  4. 4. Have you ever helped out a neighbor with something big?
  5. 5. Have you ever contributed money to a cause you cared about?
