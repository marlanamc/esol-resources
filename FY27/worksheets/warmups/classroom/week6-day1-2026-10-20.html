Getting Around — movement verbs for buses, trains, and streets.

1 Adjective or adverb?

Circle the correct word. An adjective describes a person, place, or thing. An adverb tells how someone does something (often -ly).

Example: She drives (slow / slowly). → slowly tells how she drives.

  1. 1. The bus driver is very (careful / carefully).
  2. 2. Look both ways and cross the street (careful / carefully).

  4. 4. The signs at the station are (clear / clearly).
  5. 5. Follow the map, and you will arrive (safe / safely).
