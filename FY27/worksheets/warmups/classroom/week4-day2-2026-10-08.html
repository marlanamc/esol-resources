Telling the story — what happened, and what was happening?

1 Circle the correct verb

Long action = was/were + -ing. Short action that interrupts it = past simple (V2). Two short actions in a row = V2 for both.

Example: I (was cooking / cooked) when the phone (was ringing / rang).

  1. 1. I (was reviewing / reviewed) my notes when the teacher (was coming / came) in.
  2. 2. She (was navigating / navigated) the website when her computer (was stopping / stopped).
  3. 3. My son (logged in / was logging in) when the internet (went / was going) out.
  4. 4. Last night, I (was completing / completed) the quiz, and then I (was going / went) to bed.
  5. 5. While I (accessed / was accessing) my email, I (was seeing / saw) a strange message.
2 Two truths and a lie

Example: I lived in Brazil. I had a dog named Max. I was a pilot. (The lie: I was never a pilot!)
