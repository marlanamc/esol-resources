UNIT 1: GETTING TO KNOW YOU

Speaking Assessment 1: Questions

  1. What is your name? How do you spell it?
  2. Where are you from?
  3. What do you do for work? Or what do you want to do for work?
  4. What is one thing you want to do this year?
  5. What is something that was difficult for you, but you did it?
  6. What do you like about this class so far?
