Too Much Time on Our Phones?
Based on a Pew Research Center survey, September 2026

Do you check1 your phone in the morning? You are not alone. A new survey2 from the Pew Research Center asked almost 10,000 adults in the United States about their phones. More than half said they3 spend too much time on their phones.

Phones are a part of daily life. About one in four adults said they are on their phones “almost constantly.” That means almost all the time! Most other adults use their phones several times a day.

Age makes a big4 difference. Seven out of ten adults under 30 said they spend too much time on their phones. For adults 30 to 49, it was about two out of three. About half of people ages 50 to 64 said the same. For people 65 and older, it was only one in four.

Many people want to change5. In the past year, 45 percent of adults tried to use their phones less. Was it easy? Not really. Only one in four of them said they were very successful. About half were a little successful. The rest did not change much.

Why is it so hard? A phone is a useful6 tool7. We use it to call our8 families, find jobs, pay bills, and learn English. We need it every day. But a9 phone can also steal10 our time. Apps like TikTok and Instagram are fun, and the11 videos never stop. One video becomes ten videos, and ten minutes becomes an hour. Using a phone late at night can hurt our sleep12, too.

So what can we do? Here are some ideas. Turn off notifications for apps you don’t need. Put your phone in another room at night. Use the “screen time” setting to see your hours every day. Some people set a timer before they open TikTok.

What about you? How many hours do you spend on your phone every day? Do you want to change?

Source: Pew Research Center, “About half of Americans say they spend too much time on their phone,” Sept. 1, 2026. Adapted for English learners.

Phones: Too Much?

Many people use their phones every day. A new survey asks adults in the United States about their phones.

About half of the adults say, “I use my phone too much.” Young adults say it more. Seven out of ten young adults say “too much.” Only one in four older adults say it.

Many people want to change. They try to use their phones less. It is not easy! Only one in four people change a lot.

A phone is a useful tool. We call our families. We find jobs. We pay bills. We learn English. But phones can steal our time. The videos never stop! Phones at night can hurt our sleep.

Here are some ideas. Turn off notifications. Put your phone in another room at night. Look at your screen time every day.

What about you? How many hours do you use your phone every day?
